# iPhone Duo design

> **What:** the per-pose layout decisions for iPhone Duo, which pages get list/detail, the `App/Layout` infrastructure, the root integration plan and the baseline breakages. **Read when:** building or reviewing any Duo layout. Runtime facts, APIs and simulator controls: [platforms/apple/duo.md](../../platforms/apple/duo.md).

## Principles (Apple HIG first, Fluent second)

1. **Size classes, safe areas and reserved regions only.** Compact = outer display, regular = inner display. No device names, fixed widths, `userInterfaceIdiom` or pixel detectors; never port the web's `probableDuoDisplay` heuristic.
2. **Keep the system vertical bar.** Standard `TabView`/`NavigationStack`/`.toolbar` get it for free. Never hand-build a rail or tab lane, and never `toolbarVerticalBehavior(.disabled)`: HIG says "don't override the default bar placement". The web's 3-tab Duo landscape rail (`FortniteFestivalWeb/src/components/shell/mobile/BottomNav.tsx:72-83`) was a browser workaround. Natively, the system's automatic compression keeps the tab bar and overflows toolbar items.
3. **One hierarchy on both displays.** Folded shows one level; unfolded adds a level (list + detail), like Mail. Same tabs, same toolbar actions, same state across fold and unfold. This matches Fluent's dual-screen *list-detail* pattern, with one pane per side of the fold.
4. **Custom overlays avoid reserved regions.** Overlays laid out outside the safe area (drawer, full-width banners, pinned footers) inset by `DeviceLayout.overlayInsets` (camera occlusion plus safe area); overlays inside a page's content safe area, which already ends at the vertical bar (the Songs A–Z scrubber), add only `DeviceLayout.cutoutInsets`, since `overlayInsets` there counts the bar twice (B4). Both stay out of `foldFrame`. The drawer panel insets only by the leading bar and the camera (`cutoutInsets`) at its top and bottom, like iPhone's, and its scrim covers the whole window, the vertical bar included (#339; [pattern R16](../../patterns/page-tools-and-nav-chrome.md)).

Owner: *"On Duo, keep Compete button in unfolded nav bar vertical; remove Rivals/Leaderboards. In unfolded portrait, make that consistent."* HIG Designing for iPhone Duo: "Preserve functionality, element state, hierarchy, and access across displays/poses" (should); HIG Tab bars: "use as few tabs as the hierarchy needs" (should). Why `TabView` rather than a sections sidebar when unfolded: HIG asks that controls keep their relative positions across poses, and the inner display keeps the vertical bar in landscape exactly where the folded tabs were. - **Fold crash.** Folding from inner portrait crashed in `-[UITabBarController _tabs_rebuildTabBarItemsAnimated:]` (`insertObject:atIndex:` out of bounds): the regular → compact tab set changed in the same SwiftUI update that pushed the new size class into the tab bar controller. Folding from inner landscape did not crash. `FestivalShellContent` now applies a section-set change one run-loop turn later (`ShellPresentation.applying`); the sidebar shell never defers.
- Statistics keeps two ~300 pt columns in inner portrait with legible charts (R3 capture; P1 closes R3). In book pose every grid's centre gutter now sits on the fold ([hinge-columns](../../patterns/hinge-columns.md), #343; P2 overridden by the owner).
- **Fold with a split open (#346).** Folding from inner landscape with Song Detail's full leaderboard open in the split left the outer display black until unfolded. In one animated update, the window changed size class and display, the open item was pushed onto the leading stack and the trailing pane's stack was removed. Rule: a split never changes shape in the same update as the window. When a fold, rotation or resize changes whether the window allows a split, `OnDemandSplitStack` keeps its current shape and applies the new one a run-loop turn later, without animation (`OnDemandSplitPolicy.defersWindowChange` / `appliedGeometry`). Folding pushes the open item in one column; unfolding splits again with the same item. The container's first measurement applies at once. Covered by `foldingAnOpenSplitPushesTheItemAndUnfoldingSplitsAgain` (hosted, inside `FestivalShellContent`; folds and unfolds twice and requires painted content on the pending frame, before a main-queue barrier, and on the applied one) and `windowChangesApplyOneTurnLater`. The live fold pose can't be scripted without Device Hub Accessibility permission; the iPad split → compact → refill was re-checked live twice on 2026-10-07.

## List/detail pages (regular width)

Superseded 2026-10-04 by [split-view.md](split-view.md) (D1/J3/P4 and the auto-selected always-on columns are gone): list pages split **on demand** in inner **landscape** only, with the divider on the hinge (`DeviceLayout.splitHinge`: the fold, the inactive division, else the inner display's middle); inner portrait and folded push. No line is drawn on the hinge band ([split-panes](../../patterns/split-panes.md) R1, #344). The vertical bar stays inside the trailing pane. Controls stay with the pane they affect (HIG, Mail example): each pane has its own navigation bar.

**Landed 2026-09-28 (Lane W3):** Leaderboards overview (instrument + band cards) and Statistics/Player Profile (per-instrument stats-card-plus-charts tiles) switch to a 2-column `LazyVGrid` when `deviceLayout.widthClass == .regular`; each stays its existing single-column `LazyVStack`/plain stack otherwise, so iPhone and folded Duo are unchanged (`LeaderboardsScreen.swift`'s `regularWidthColumns`/`cards`, `PlayerProfileContent.swift`'s `instrumentGridColumns`/`instrumentTile(_:instrument:)`). Settings intentionally does **not** get a 2-column grid — its rows are label/control pairs, not dashboard cards, so a wide row just reads worse split in half. Instead `SettingsScreen.swift` gained `ReadableWidthContainer`, capping its content to ~680pt and centering it on regular width. **Superseded (#355, agent decision):** the owner asked for two-column Settings when unfolded; in inner landscape the sections now flow column by column into two balanced columns of at most 680 pt each, meeting at the hinge; portrait, folded and accessibility text sizes stay one readable column ([wide-columns](../../patterns/wide-columns.md) R7). Not yet captured natively (Device Hub unfold still needed); verified via hosted tests injecting `\.deviceLayout` at a regular-width size would be the next step if Device Hub time opens up.

Rules (`App/Layout/DualSource{Policy,Layout}.swift`, `Common/HorizontalCarousel.swift`):

- **Gate.** Only with `DualSourcePolicy.isEnabled`: `DeviceLayout.contentArrangement == .dualSource` ⇔ Duo inner display (pose `.unfolded`/`.partiallyFolded`), portrait, fold (if any) horizontal, not the sidebar shell. It also turns off list/detail (`ListDetailPolicy.usesSplit`) and keeps the compact section set, so Compete (the two-source hub) is a tab in portrait; landscape keeps Leaderboards + Rivals.
- **Split.** At the fold: primary ends at `fold.minY`, secondary starts at `fold.maxY` (a hairline division widens to 8 pt, centred). Flat: 58 % / 12 pt / 42 %. A fold that leaves < 120 pt on a side falls back to the flat split; a container < 252 pt shows one region. First placement is not animated; fold ↔ flat animates the divider (none with Reduce Motion).
- **Identity.** The primary keeps its identity across poses (only the secondary appears); Compete is the exception (its primary becomes a carousel). Pages that move content into the secondary (Profile graphs) read `DualSourcePolicy.isActive` to avoid showing it twice.
- **Selection.** `dualSourceSelection(_:section:)` reuses the list/detail `ListDetailLink` contract: while split, rows write a local selection shown by the secondary; otherwise the environment passes through (a landscape split keeps its own).
- **Carousel.** Snaps per card (`.viewAligned`), cards ≥ 300 pt (1 per outer-width region, 2 on the inner display), each card scrolls vertically if taller than the region, content clipped out of the safe area. Indicator: dots for 2–10 finite cards, else "n / N(+)"; one adjustable VoiceOver element ("Graphs, Page 2 of 6"). Endless sources pass `hasMore` + `onNearEnd`. Automation id `fst.carousel.<title-slug>`. Entrance ([load-transition](../../patterns/load-transition.md) R5): without `entranceIndex` the track block-fades on appear; with it (Compete, #354) each card fades at stagger `entranceIndex + n` and the indicator with the first card, inside the page's `festivalNestedFadeInScope()`, and a swipe or a card's own scroll rushes the rest (`festivalFadeInRushOnScroll`). `DualSourcePane(entranceIndex:)` fades its header the same way.
- **Secondary chrome.** `DualSourcePane` (Title Case header + optional View All push (`SectionViewAllLink`, `fst.dual.<id>.view-all`) onto the page's own stack; `fst.dual.<id>`), `DualSourceMessage` for loading/empty/paused/error. Secondaries own their loads; no navigation containers inside.
- **Shop picks.** `SuggestionsViewModel.restrictCandidates(to:publicationId:)` filters the generator's candidate songs client-side (no service change). A Shop feed from a different observed publication than the catalogue **pauses** the pane ("Picks Paused"), never re-labels rows (Shop invariant, [AGENTS.md](../../../AGENTS.md)).
- **Evidence.** Device Hub poses need the Accessibility grant (not yet given), so native captures use `FST_DEBUG_DUO_POSE` ([platforms/apple/duo.md](../../platforms/apple/duo.md#simulator-alias-duo)) on the outer display: correct regions and behaviour at outer-display size inside the vertical-bar chrome, not inner-display pixels. ## Toolbar rules (all Duo poses)

- **Page titles** (issue #341; [page-tools-and-nav-chrome](../../patterns/page-tools-and-nav-chrome.md) R14, agent decision): every page keeps its system navigation title in every pose, folded and unfolded. Inner portrait has a horizontal bar, so a root's large title collapses into it as on iPhone. Under the vertical bar (folded, inner landscape) the title sits at the top of the page and scrolls away with the content even in `.inline` mode (measured folded, iOS 27.1); the selected rail tab or Back keeps the location. Don't hide the bar, switch display modes per pose or pin a custom title. Item Shop, pushed from Songs, keeps the roots' large title (#375: same 34 pt in every pose; a title only looks bigger beside 17 pt inline or collapsed titles, or on the narrower outer display). Tested per pose by `DuoPageTitleJourneyTests` (see the pattern's guards).
- Every toolbar item is a `Label(title, systemImage:)`: vertical placement needs the icon and overflow needs the title. Custom-view items stay horizontal and force a top bar, so avoid them.
- Profile avatar: when `deviceLayout.sectionChrome` is `.verticalBar`, show the symbol `person.crop.circle`/`.fill` titled "Profile: <name>". Keep the monogram avatar in horizontal bars. Bell and profile carry `visibilityPriority(.high)` (iOS 27+), so page actions overflow into `…` first (✅ W1: `RootProfileButton.Presentation`). Capacity measured folded portrait: with a profile (5 tabs) the rail fits only **two** root items plus `…`, so the hamburger lands in `…` as "Menu"; anonymous (3 tabs) shows hamburger, sort, Quick Links, bell and profile. Giving the hamburger `.high` too pushed the profile into `…` instead. **Decided (operator, 2026-09-28; W3):** Bell + Profile stay visible in the 5-tab rail; the hamburger overflows into `…` as "Menu" (the drawer's View/Switch/Deselect Profile items stay reachable there, and Profile identity is the more useful glanceable item of the two). Encoded as `RootChromeRailItem` in `App/Shell/RootChrome.swift` — `FestivalRootChrome`/`FestivalRootTrailingItems` apply it as the real `visibilityPriority`, and `RootChromeRailPriorityTests` pins the ranking. Visual capture: `~/FestivalShowcase/2026-09-28/duo-w3/rail-profile-folded.png`. Issue #92 (2026-10-04): the separate Search tab leaves room for two items plus `…` even with four section tabs (Statistics leaves the compact tabs); Quick Links therefore keeps the default priority, and the folded rail shows bell, Profile and `…` (live capture, SFentonX, Year sort).
- Bell and profile glass groups: horizontal bars split them into two buttons with `ToolbarSpacer(.fixed)` (issue #14, 2026-10-01). Since issue #92 horizontal bars use two groups (page tools | bell, avatar). The vertical bar adds **no** fixed spacer at all, so page actions, Quick Links, bell and profile form one group (`RootChromeTrailingGroups.separatesAccount`; HIG iPhone Duo: "system spacing adapts, so don't add fixed spacing"). Global search is no longer a rail item: the Search tab sits separately at the end of the tab rail.
- Page actions that belong in the rail (Player profile "Select Profile"/"Switch To This Profile") add `VerticalBarActionItem` (`App/Shell/RootChrome.swift`): a titled symbol item in its own group right after Back, only while the chrome is a vertical bar; horizontal bars keep the in-content button (in-page design: Lane A2). A page's **primary** action passes `prominentFill` and is drawn `.borderedProminent` in that fill, the same as its header button, with `visibilityPriority(.high)` (`RootChromeRailItem.primaryPageAction`) so it never hides in `…`: Player Select and Switch are both accent blue in the rail (issue #351; HIG Designing for iPhone Duo: "Place primary navigation (Back/Close) at the top, then prominent actions (Done)"). Plain page actions keep a monochrome symbol and default priority.
- Every toolbar change animates in the rail, so a page must not re-render its toolbar while it is being pushed or popped. Leaderboards › Player › Back (operator bug) was bisected with `drive --record` (`.visual-output/duo-w1/jitter/`): Full Rankings › Back and Settings › Licenses › Back are clean.
