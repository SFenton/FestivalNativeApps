- **Two columns when full width (#353):** a Solo board pushed full width (not beside Song Detail): full-width boards pair their rows in two columns in wide landscape ([wide-columns](../../patterns/wide-columns.md) R8, agent decision #353; owner may override with `/choose`): iPad landscape, the unfolded Duo in landscape and a Mac page wider than tall and at least 684 pt wide show rank rows row-major (#1 | #2, #3 | #4) through `WideColumns.indexedRows` + `WideColumnsRow` (`HingeRow`, so the gutter sits on the Duo hinge in book pose and at the free space's midpoint flat, owner #361), with each column fitted to the section's one column plan at `WideColumns.columnPageWidth`. The pinned selected row and the pager stay one full-width bar under both columns, and a selected-row reveal scrolls to the pair's row (`WideColumns.rowStart`). Portrait, iPhone, a folded Duo and a split's sub-page (`\.splitPaneSubPage`) stay one column. Rotation re-pairs the loaded rows without reloading. HIG Designing for iPhone Duo: "Expand the existing layout with space"; HIG Layout: "Choose layout from size classes". The pinned footer shares the per-column plan. Same code on the Duo and the Mac.
