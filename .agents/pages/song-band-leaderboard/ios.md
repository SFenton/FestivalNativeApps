# Song band leaderboard — iPhone notes

> **What:** iPhone implementation state and decisions for `/songs/:songId/bands/:bandType`. **Read when:** changing this page on iPhone. Behavior: [spec.md](spec.md).

Source: `FortniteFestivalWeb/src/pages/leaderboard/band/SongBandLeaderboardPage.tsx`, `.../components/bands/SongBandScoreFooter.tsx`. Service: `FSTService/Api/LeaderboardEndpoints.cs:98` (`/api/leaderboard/{songId}/bands/{bandType}`) — pure, keyless `GET` (`MetaDatabase.GetSongBandLeaderboard`/`GetSongBandLeaderboardEntryForAccount`/`...ForTeam`, only `SELECT`s).

- **Song header, bar title and backdrop (issue #317, pattern [song-leaderboard-header](../../patterns/song-leaderboard-header.md)):** the page shares the solo board's song header (`SongHeaderRow` with `SongLeaderboardBoardLine`; since #315, see below) as the first item of its `ScrollView`, above the reload gate (art, title, artist, then "Duos"/"Trios"/"Quads", plus " · N entries" when the response's `showLeaderboardEntryTotals` is true; no icon, like the web `SongInfoHeader` `subtitle2`), 20 pt below the bar and 12 pt above the first card. `navigationTitle` is the song title (back menu and window title only); `SongBarTitleToolbarItem` keeps the bar empty until the header scrolls away, then shows art, title and band size. The backdrop is `festivalBackground(.song(song.albumArt))`, not the carousel. A band-size, page or selected-player change keeps the header and reloads only the rows below it (pattern R5; the review of PR #311 found the header vanished while the gate reloaded). The board line reads the new size at once and adds its total once that size answers; the gate is sized to the rest of the visible page so the spinner centres under the header, and a reload scrolls back to the header like the web, except a page change while the header is under the bar: the gate then grows to the whole visible page (`rowsAtTop`) until the new rows are revealed and the page scrolls to the first row, so the bar title stays and the header never flashes back (#316, pattern R3). It used to read "<Band> Scores" over the animated carousel with no header. HIG Toolbars (should): "you can omit [the title] when content supplies context". IDs: `fst.song-band-leaderboard.header`, `fst.song-band-leaderboard.pinned-title`. Tests: `songBandLeaderboardScreenLoadsRows` / `…SwitchesBandType` (header and board line), `songBandLeaderboardKeepsTheHeaderWhileAnotherBandSizeLoads` (hosts `SongBandLeaderboardContent` on a test-owned band size, holds the Trios response and checks the header reads "Trios" with no stale total while the spinner runs), `songLeaderboardBoardLineAddsTotalsOnlyWhenAsked`, `songBandLeaderboardDecodesEntryTotalsSwitch`.
- Implemented: paginated `ScrollView`/`LazyVStack` of `SongBandPreviewRow` glass cards, the same card as Song Detail's band previews (web `PlayerBandCard` + `SongBandScoreFooter` on both pages; issue #90 replaced the thinner `List` cells, 4 pt vertical padding, with it). Not a `List`: the cards are `NavigationLink`s and a `List` would add a second disclosure chevron. Reuses `RankingsPagerView`/`RankLoadState`. A toolbar `Menu` switches `BandType` in place (mirrors `BandRankingsScreen`'s `bandTypeMenu`) rather than re-navigating, since the web page also lets the visitor switch band size without leaving. Cards navigate to `AppRoute.band(bandId:name:bandType:teamKey:)`; the selected player's band is highlighted when it is on the page (`SongBandLeaderboardResponse.isSelected`, by `bandId`, else `teamKey`), and so is the focused band from Song Detail (issue #307, see below).
- **Selected player's band footer (issue #306):** with a selected player, the page reads `?accountId=<selected id>` (query only; no headers; bypasses the snapshot cache) and the service adds `selectedPlayerEntry` (the player's best band of this size; pure `SELECT`). Web `SongBandLeaderboardPage` pins it in `FixedLeaderboardPlayerFooter`; native pins one Solo-style row (the Solo board's shared `SelectedScoreFooterRow(starsAfterScore: true)`: `SongLeaderboardEntryRow(isPlayer: true)` on a selected `RankingRowSurface`) above the pager in the same `safeAreaInset(edge: .bottom)` chrome as the Solo chart and Full Rankings, with rows fading out under it (`bottomChromeFadeMask`). `SongBandLeaderboardEntry.footerLeaderboardEntry` maps the band row: name = members joined with " + " (the teamKey if none), accuracy, FC, stars, season. Season shows from 520 pt and stars from 768 pt of width (web shows both only on desktop), via `LeaderboardRowColumns.fit`. Long names scroll with the shared `LeaderboardNameText` marquee and tail-truncate under Reduce Motion (issue #292). Tapping it follows the Solo footer's jump-or-open rule (issue #307, below): Band Detail (`AppRoute.band`, web `getBandProfileRoute`; native has no separate selected-band profile) while the band's row is on this page, otherwise a jump to that row. It is hidden with no selected player, no band of that size, or while a response for another size/player is shown (`chromePayload` matches `bandType` and `accountId`). Reduce Transparency and Increase Contrast get an opaque backing.
  - The fade mask must read `bottomChromeTop` in `body` and take it as a parameter (`bottomChromeFadeMask(chromeTop:)`): read only inside the mask's `GeometryReader`, the mask stayed opaque and rows drew behind the footer and pager on iPhone (the Full Rankings bug, issue #294).
  - HIG: `layout` ("Respect system safe areas, margins, and guides"), `designing-for-iphone-duo` ("Use safe areas so controls don't cover content"), `motion`/accessibility ("When Reduce Motion is on, reduce automatic and repetitive animation"), `materials` ("Let content scroll and peek through while preserving control and navigation legibility"). macOS keeps the Solo bottom placement; the layout page's advice to keep critical information away from a window's bottom is a recommendation, and the pager already lives there.
  - iPad, iPhone Duo and macOS (2026-10-05, live `Dance Therapy`, `SFentonX` Duos #1,518): iPad portrait shows the full name with season, stars and accuracy; the Duo outer display (folded) moved the pager into the side rail (superseded by #345: it now stays under the board) and keeps the footer above the bottom safe area; the hosted Mac-width render (1000 pt) shows season and stars. The Duo inner display, iPad landscape and the running Mac app could not be captured on this host (Device Hub pose/rotation needs the Accessibility permission; `screencapture -l` needs Screen Recording).
- **Song header (issue #315, pattern [song-header](../../patterns/song-header.md)):** the cards scroll under the shared `SongHeaderRow` (80 pt art; title `.title3.bold()`, artist and band-size line, each one full-width `MarqueeText` line that scrolls only on overflow, in lockstep), like the web's `SongInfoHeader` (`collapsed`, `subtitle2` = band size, plus "· N entries" when the response's `showLeaderboardEntryTotals` is true: `SongLeaderboardBoardLine`). Once it scrolls under the bar, `SongBarTitleToolbarItem` shows the shared `SongBarTitle` (28 pt art, title, band size caption; ID `fst.song-band-leaderboard.pinned-title`); before that an empty unspoken principal placeholder keeps the navigation title out of the inline bar. The header is one VoiceOver heading (`fst.song-band-leaderboard.header`) that reads the title once. Reduce Motion tail-truncates; accessibility sizes wrap in-page and stay one line in the bar. The bar title is as wide as the bar leaves between its items (no fixed cap), and the ScrollView's offset decides when it pins (`songHeaderScrollAway`). The backdrop is the song's dimmed cover (song-header agent decision with options and HIG Materials; #317's song-leaderboard-header R4). The #317 `SongLeaderboardHeader`/`SongLeaderboardPinnedTitle` were folded into these shared components when #315 merged. The web title link to Song Details is not ported (as on Song Leaderboard: Back returns there). HIG `typography.md` (*should*): "Keep text truncation to a minimum as font size increases"; `accessibility.md`: "reduce automatic and repetitive animation". Tests: hosted `SongHeaderTextTests` (row: one line, full width, AX wrap, one heading, ScrollView scroll-away, bar-title width), iOS `SongHeaderJourneyTests` (landscape pinned title spans the bar) and `BandsRenderTests.songBandLeaderboardScreenLoadsRows` (title read once). The pinned bar title is iOS/iPadOS only; the Mac keeps its window title so trailing toolbar actions never move to overflow (song-header R4, macOS decision).
- Not implemented: the instrument-combo filter (`BandFilterPill`/`BandInstrumentFilterModal`, `?combo=`) — a secondary refinement deferred rather than guessed at — and the selected *band* identity (`selectedBandTeamKey`; natives have no selected band, so only `accountId` is sent); the `combo` parameter is already threaded through `FestivalAPI.songBandLeaderboard` so a future picker only needs UI.

- **Bottom fade above the pager (issue #305):** the pager was a `VStack` sibling under the `ScrollView`, so cards stopped at a hard edge while Song Leaderboard's rows fade, which read as "no fade without a player score". The pager now sits in `.safeAreaInset(edge: .bottom)` outside the reload gate, built from the last loaded page while it answers the current band size and selected player (`chromePayload`, which also feeds the #306 footer), and the cards use the shared `bottomChromeFade` (`BottomChromeFade.swift`: 36 pt `ScrollEdgeFade.bottom` mask ending at the chrome top measured in `fst.song-band-leaderboard.page`, shrinking to nothing at the end of the list). The stack pads its bottom by one 6 pt card gap and `PinnedChromeSpacing` gives the pager no top padding, so the last card rests 6 pt above the pager, fully opaque. The Duo vertical bar keeps the same pager and fade since #345 (beside the bar when unfolded). HIG Layout: "Prefer scroll-edge effects to solid/semi-opaque backgrounds beneath controls"; HIG Scroll views: "With custom bars, you might add one manually if needed" (`apple-hig` `references/hig/layout.md`, `references/hig/scroll-views.md`). Checked live on iPhone 17 Pro (Zombie, Duos: top, mid-scroll and end of page 1); hosted `BandsRenderTests` unchanged.
- **Row stagger and band jump (issue #323):** cards stagger in once per revealed page (`festivalFadeIn(staggerIndex:)` in the page's `festivalScrollFadeInScope(fadeScope, resetKey: rowsReveal)`, re-armed by the gate's `onReveal`), no longer as one block. Opening a band's row (`SongBandRowFocus`) scrolls through `SelectedRowReveal` with the scope and the card's index: the scroll waits for that card's entrance (web `navToBand`), and the cards it reaches fade in with the rush ([load-transition](../../patterns/load-transition.md) R5). Because the gate sits inside the scroll view, its reveal grows the scroll content over the fade; the jump also waits for that to finish, or it stops at the half-grown content's end (#327). Tests: `selectedRowRevealFadesTheRowItReaches`, `fadeScopeAutomaticScrollWaitsForTheContentReveal`.
- **Load/reload fade (issue #71):** The band leaderboard runs the shared `FestivalReloadGate` on load and on a band size or page change: old results leave at once, the spinner fades in, holds ≥400 ms, fades out (500 ms) and the results fade/stagger in; Reduce Motion swaps instantly. See [iPhone motion](../../design/apple/iphone.md#motion).
- **Selected band focus (issue #307):** `.songBandLeaderboard(song, bandType:, page:, focus:)` opens the board at `page` with a `SongBandRowFocus` (equal non-empty `bandId`, or same size and `teamKey`; web `isSameSongBandEntry`). The focused band's card gets the purple highlight and is scrolled to the centre once its page loads (`SelectedRowReveal`; instant under Reduce Motion); changing band size drops the scroll. Song Detail's appended selected-band row opens it this way, like the solo spotlight row (see [song-leaderboard/ios.md](../song-leaderboard/ios.md) for the shared rule).
- **Selected-band footer action (issue #307):** the #306 footer follows the Solo footer (pattern [leaderboard-row](../../patterns/leaderboard-row.md) R5): `SongBandRowNavigation.footerAction(for:pageEntries:)` → `.openProfile` while the row is on this page (`NavigationLink` to Band Detail, `fst.song-band-leaderboard.spotlight-open`, label "Your band's rank, 29th. Open band."); `.jump(page:)` otherwise (while the next page loads, the band's rank against the requested page decides, like the Solo footer) (`jump(to:focusing:)` → that page, row revealed and highlighted; `fst.song-band-leaderboard.spotlight-jump`, "Your band's rank, 29th. Jump to your band's position."). Labels come from `SelectedRowAction.footerLabel(for:rank:)`, shared with the Solo footer. The web footer always opens the band; the native jump is the deliberate #307 rule shared with the Solo board.
