# Song Detail — Windows notes

> **What:** what the Windows Song Detail page implements and its open gaps. **Read when:** changing `windows/Festival.App/Pages/SongDetailPage*`, `SongDetailViewModel` or `SongPathsView*`. Behavior: [spec.md](spec.md).

## Implemented

- Route `AppRoute.SongDetail(songId, instrument?)`, resolved against the current catalogue (missing → not-found status).
- Header: 128 px art, title (heading 1), `artist · year · duration`, **Paths** (brand-purple accent) and **Item Shop** (official link via `Launcher`, validated `https://www.fortnite.com/item-shop/jam-tracks/…` only) actions. There is no "Item Shop: …" chip (operator 2026-09-28): the Item Shop button's fill breathes in the status colour (`Controls/ShopPulseFill`, web `shopBreathe*`: 3 s ease-in-out from the `#F5121826` surface to gold `#CFA500` New, red `#EF4444` Leaving Tomorrow or green `#1E7F46` in the Shop) while Shop highlighting is on; highlighting off, Reduce Motion / Animation effects off or a hidden window hold the colour still. The UIA name carries the status ("Open in Item Shop, Leaving Tomorrow"). A failed Shop read shows a warning `InfoBar` (`fst.song-detail.shop-error`); hidden Shop shows neither.
- Pinned header: once the full header scrolls away, a compact bar (40 epx art, marquee title/subtitle, **Paths** command) stays at the top (`fst.song-detail.pinned-header`, `fst.song-detail.pinned-paths`), on the near-opaque `FSTPinnedHeaderBrush` (no blur).
- Intensity card for **every charted** chart (hidden ones included), web `IntensityCard` cells: 36 epx icon + bars (name in the tooltip and UIA name), three per row (3×3 for nine charts) at compact/medium and all on one row when they fit (`SongDetailLayout.IntensityColumns`).
- At most **two** leaderboard cards per row, even on wide windows (web `isTwoCol`); two from 320 epx each, so portrait tablet (800) and snap halves already show two (operator 6.31).
- **Load choreography** (web `useLoadPhase` + `useStagger`, operator 6.41; `Domain/SongDetailReveal`): the page keeps its spinner until the song, the player's scores, their song history and every chart's top rows have settled, fades the spinner out (500 ms), then fades up header → Intensity (100 ms) → Score History (150 ms) → Leaderboards and card rows (300 ms + 150 ms per grid row). Loads under 150 ms (cached) or with motion off reveal at once. Content is transparent, not collapsed, during the fade so cards realize and measure first.
- **Score History** (web `ScoreHistoryChart` in `GraphCard`, operator 6.39; `SongScoreHistoryViewModel`, `Controls/SongScoreHistoryChart`): selected player only, between Intensity and Leaderboards. One read, `GET /api/player/{id}/history?songId=` (all charts), filtered by Filter Invalid Scores (`maxScore × (1 + leeway/100)`). Card: required [Instrument Selector](../../controls/instrument-selector/windows.md) over the visible charts with rows (route chart, else Lead, else first), title + "Select a bar…", accuracy bars (red→green, gold 100% FC, purple stroke when selected) on a right 0–100% axis with the score as a blue line on a nice four-step left axis, `M/D/YY` labels, legend; bars page by width (96 epx min bar + 8 gap, « ‹ › » like `useChartPagination`) and a selected bar shows its detail row. Below: sort menu (native, keeps the old page's Date/Score/Accuracy/Season), top five by score with the best in the web purple, then **View all scores** expands in place. Hidden without a player, for unregistered players (404) or with no rows; 202 shows "still being prepared" + Retry; failures show the inline status. IDs keep the `fst.history.*` family (`fst.history`, `.subtitle`, `.chart` (on the plot canvas: a `UserControl` has no UIA peer), `.bar.<n>`, `.detail` (the detail row itself, `ScoreHistoryListRow.AutomationId`), `.page-*`, `.entry-*`, `.rows`, `.view-all`, `.message`, `.retry` (both Retry buttons, as Android), `.error`, `.sort.*`, `.instrument.<Solo_…>`). Bars are `ToggleButton`s (pressed = selected, so Narrator says "selected"/"not pressed"; Space/Enter toggle) and keep keyboard focus across the redraw a selection causes; each bar is a Tab stop and Left/Right move between them. Contrast themes use the `FSTChart*Brush` roles (`Themes/Styles.xaml`: WindowText axes and outlined bars, FC bars filled, Highlight score line and selection) and redraw on `UISettings.ColorValuesChanged`; axis bands, the date band and the bar slots grow with the Windows text size (`TextScaleLayout.Factor`) so the rotated axis titles never overlap the tick labels at 200%.
- **Instrument switch** (issue #61, iOS #31; `Domain/ScoreHistorySwap`): the selector updates at once while `ScoreHistorySwapper` (driven from `SongDetailPage`) fades the chart, detail row, pager slot, top rows and View All out (150 ms composition opacity), swaps the VM chart, then fades in (250 ms); instant with Animation effects off, in-app Reduce Motion or `--reduce-motion`; a newer pick cancels the running swap and returning to the drawn chart just fades back in. The card keeps its size: `HistoryCard.MinHeight` is held during the swap and eased back afterwards, and `ShowPagerSlot` keeps the 40 epx pager row (`HistoryPagerSlot`) while any selectable chart pages. Verified with Core tests (`Swap_*`, `Swapper_*`, `PagerSlot_*`) and a UIA journey (sort button stays at the same y across Lead → Bass, mid-swap and rapid switches). The plot's UIA name follows `ChartSummary` (set after the bars), so it never reads the previous chart after a switch or page (fixed in issue #198).
- The `/songs/:id/:instrument/history` route (`AppRoute.PlayerHistory`) opens **Song Detail** with that chart selected and scrolled to Score History (`MainWindow.PageFor`); the separate `PlayerHistoryPage` is no longer routed.
- Rows are the shared `LeaderboardEntryRow` (operator batch 7.7): no card around the rows, each a frosted 48 epx row (`fst.song-detail.preview-row.<instrument>.<accountId>`) with rank, name, season (from 520 epx), score, accuracy badge and an in-card chevron (operator 7.3); loading, empty and failed charts keep their own card. Top-ten rows open the player (`AppRoute.Player`), the selected player's row eleven opens the full board at their page (`?page=`, web spotlight row); the card shares one rank and score width with row eleven. Each row with a destination is one UIA `Button` (Invoke; click, Enter and Space open it; Alt+Left returns), and each card's rows are one Tab stop with the arrow keys moving between them (`ItemsRepeater`). A row without an account (empty `accountId`, `…preview-row.<instrument>.rank-<n>`) has no chevron, isn't hit-testable or tabbable, and its peer (`Controls/LeaderboardRowButton`) is `Text` with no Invoke pattern (issue #63; checked with a fixture row with an empty `accountId`). Score-history rows use the same control as labelled rows (date instead of rank, personal best purple).
- **Season in Score History (issue #62):** list and top-score rows show the season only from a 520 epx row (`LeaderboardColumnLayout.Fit`). The tapped bar's detail row (`ScoreHistoryListRow.IsDetail`, max 560 epx) pins it (`ILeaderboardScoreRow.PinsSeason` → `Fit(pinSeason: true)`), so it shows at any width and is never dropped for space, matching the web `renderDetailCard`. Tests: `PinnedSeason_ShowsAtAnyWidthAndIsNeverDroppedForSpace`, `SongScoreHistoryTests`, `LeaderboardUnifyTests`.
- **Narrator reads a shown season (issue #262):** each row is one UIA stop with Raw parts, so the row name carries the season while it is on screen and drops it when it hides (`ILeaderboardScoreRow.SeasonShownAnnouncement`, applied by `LeaderboardEntryRow` after each column fit, so it follows resizes). History rows already read it at every width. The season text is `fst.score.season.<badge key>` (`LeaderboardScoreRowIds.Season`; e.g. `fst.score.season.preview.Solo_Guitar.<accountId>`, `fst.score.season.history.<stamp>[.detail]`). Journey: `tools/windows/journeys/song-season.json`.
- Rows: rank, name, score, then the accuracy badge (web `AccuracyDisplay`, `Controls/ScoreBadge`): a full combo is the gold skewed outline badge; others a red→green tinted pill. No separate FC chip.
- Leaderboards: one card per **visible** charted instrument, all filled by one `GET /api/leaderboard/{song}/all?top=10` (plus `leeway` when Filter Invalid Scores is on; web `getAllLeaderboards`). A failed read becomes each card's inline error with Retry (per-chart read); a 404 (older service, `tools/mock_service.py`) falls back to per-chart reads. The icon + name header sits **above** the card (`Controls/CardHeader`) with "N total entries" as its subtitle when the service allows totals (`showLeaderboardEntryTotals`). Rows are the top ten, then the selected player's own row as **row eleven** when they rank outside it (built from their profile score); a player inside the top ten is highlighted in place. **View Full Leaderboard** is a full-width purple accent button below the rows (`fst.song-detail.view-all.<instrument>`). A chart with no scores says "No scores recorded yet" as the header subtitle (web `songDetail.noScores`), the web's `noScoresSubtitle` sentence in the card and no View Full Leaderboard. Cards carry no "Your score…" text (operator 6.38); the selected player's row (in place or row eleven) uses the web's purple highlight (`purpleHighlight`/`purpleHighlightBorder`) with **bold** rank and name (`LeaderboardEntry isPlayer`, operator 6.42; `Controls/SongDetailVisuals`).
- **Band previews** (issue #64, iOS #34; `SongBandPreviewViewModel`, `Controls/SongBandPreviewRowView`): after the instrument cards, one section per band size (Duos, Trios, Quads) in the same two-column grid (`BandBoards`), all fed by **one** keyless read, `GET /api/leaderboard/{songId}/bands/all?top=10[&accountId=]` (`FestivalApiClient.GetSongBandLeaderboardsAsync`; selected player as the query `accountId` only, no selected-profile headers; the page's only band read). It replaces the old "Band Leaderboards:" link row and never holds the page spinner (sections start Loading with a `ProgressRing`). Each section: a `CardHeader` (`fst.song-detail.band-header.<type>`) with "No scores recorded yet" or, when `showLeaderboardEntryTotals`, "`N` bands"; up to ten band rows (`fst.song-detail.band-row.<type>.<i>`, 0-based) as Surface cards with rank, members with instrument icons, a chevron, then score, FC badge, accuracy pill and `StarRow`, each opening Band Detail (`AppRoute.Band`); the selected player's band (`selectedBandEntry ?? selectedPlayerEntry`, same `bandId` or `bandType`+`teamKey`, `SongBandPreview.IsSameBand`) gets the purple player-row brushes in place or is appended after the ten (`fst.song-detail.band-selected.<type>`); then **View Full Leaderboard** (spoken "View full `<type>` leaderboard", `fst.song-detail.band-view-all.<type>`, same IDs as Android) to `AppRoute.SongBandLeaderboard`. Empty sizes show the web's `noBandScoresSubtitle` sentence (`fst.song-detail.band-empty.<type>`) and no View Full; a failed or invalid read shows the inline status with Retry per section (`fst.song-detail.band-retry.<type>`). Narrator reads one button per row ("Rank 1, A + B, 94,500 points, 96.5% accuracy, full combo, 5 stars", prefixed "Your band, " when selected). Responses are validated (`SongBandLeaderboardsResponse.Validate`: same song, known unique sizes, `count` = rows ≤ `top`, rows filed under their own size) and reloaded when the selected player changes (stale responses dropped). Quick Links gain `band-<type>` rows after the instruments. Tests: `SongBandPreviewTests` (Core); UIA journey on the mock (`/songs/fixture-pulse` with `fixture-player-1`: rows, appended selected band, empty Trios/Quads, View Full → song band leaderboard, Back, row → Band Detail).
- Paths: modal `ContentDialog` (focus contained and restored) sized to the window; see [chopt-paths/windows.md](../../controls/chopt-paths/windows.md).

## Validation (issue #195)

Checked 2026-10 with the winui-design and winui-code-review skills, `a11y_matrix.py` (fixture) and the live public service (SFentonX, "Never Back Down"). Axe.Windows 0 errors in every row.

| Configuration | Finding |
|---|---|
| Compact (500 epx), snap-left (640) | Single column. **Fixed:** Tab skipped the lower cards because `ItemsRepeater` virtualized them (unrealized elements aren't in the Tab order); the card grids no longer virtualize and the row repeaters keep `VerticalCacheLength="20"`. **Fixed:** Trios/Quads cards were clipped because `UniformGridLayout` sizes every card from the first one (band rows are taller); Boards and BandBoards now use `LeaderboardsCardGridLayout` (row height = tallest card). |
| Medium (900), wide (1280, clamped by the 300% 4K desktop), maximized | Two cards per row; correct. |
| Keyboard only | Header → Paths → Item Shop → Quick Links → history chart picker → bars → Sort → each card (rows are one Tab stop, arrows between rows) → View Full → band sections → pinned Paths → Back. **Fixed:** Shift+Tab could leave the focused control under the pinned header (WCAG 2.4.11). `OnContentBringIntoViewRequested` now widens each focus scroll by `SongDetailLayout.RevealBelowPinnedHeader` (header height, at least 64 epx). Esc closes Paths and focus returns. |
| High Contrast (Desert, Night sky) | **Fixed:** the Item Shop button used a fixed dark surface, and the chart axes (20% white) and selected stroke (purple) weren't system colours. They now use `FSTShopButtonSurfaceBrush`, `FSTChartAxisBrush` (WindowText) and `FSTChartSelectedStrokeBrush` (Highlight). Bars get a WindowText outline, and the chart and Shop fill redraw on `ColorValuesChanged`. Data hues stay (accuracy colours, gold FC, blue score line): the legend and every bar's UIA name carry the meaning. |
| Light and dark system theme | Same rendering, a deliberate deviation: the app is dark only ([design/windows.md](../../design/windows.md#content-branded-fluent-tokens)). |
| Text 200% | Header, rows and cards reflow (Intensity drops to one column at compact). **Fixed:** the chart's rotated "Score"/"Accuracy" titles overlapped the tick labels (fixed 64 epx gutters). The gutters, tick offsets and date band are now measured from the scaled labels (`ScoreHistoryChartScale.AxisGutter`), and the chart re-measures on `TextScaleFactorChanged`. The shell's notification badge no longer overflows at 200% (fixed in #229, rechecked in #253). |
| Display 100% / 150% | Correct. |
| Narrator / UIA | Buttons named with state ("Open in Item Shop, Leaving Tomorrow"; band rows "Rank 1, …"; bars "date, score, accuracy"), headings and the chart summary name; reading order follows the Tab order. |

## Validation (issue #262)

Season display (#62), checked 2026-10 with the winui-design and winui-code-review skills, `a11y_matrix.py` on the fixture (`tools/windows/journeys/song-season.json`, `score-accuracy.json`) and the live public service (SFentonX, "Never Back Down"). The visual rule held everywhere. The one bug was that preview and full-leaderboard rows never read a visible season (fixed, see the #262 bullet above). The rule uses the row width, not the window width: winui-design's "Show/hide — Secondary metadata is optional at small widths" applied per row, as the web's card-width `SEASON_BREAKPOINT` does.

| Configuration | Finding |
|---|---|
| Compact (500 epx) | Preview and history rows are about 450 epx, so the season is hidden and not read. The tapped bar's detail row shows and reads it (live: "S15"). |
| 680 epx and snap-left/right (640) | One card column, so preview rows are 540–580 epx and show and read the season (live: "S4", "S10" …). Full song leaderboard rows show it from snap width up. |
| Medium (900), wide and maximized (1280) | Two card columns, so preview rows are under 520 epx and hide the season. Full-width history rows show it. |
| Live resize compact → 680 → compact | The season appears and the row name gains ", season N", then both drop again (`season-resize`). |
| Light and dark system theme | Same rendering (the app is dark only, a deliberate deviation). Axe 0. |
| High Contrast Desert | All four journeys pass. Axe 0. |
| Text 200% | The chart pages to one bar at compact. The detail row keeps the season; list rows follow the 520 rule. Axe 0. |
| Display 100% / 150% | Correct. Axe reports only the known WinUI `PopupHost` item (the bar tooltip, [windows-accessibility.md](../../testing/windows-accessibility.md) open item 8). |
| Keyboard only | Tab walk: 19–21 stops, none outside the app and no repeats. |
| Narrator / UIA | Row names read ", season N" only while it is shown (before ", you" on the selected player's row, before the stars on the full board). Season text has `fst.score.season.<badge key>`. `score-accuracy.json` passes 42/42 across 6 sizes. |
