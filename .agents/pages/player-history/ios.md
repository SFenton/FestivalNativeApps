# Player history — iPhone notes

> **What:** where the selected player's score history lives on iPhone. **Read when:** changing score history, its deep link or its endpoint. Behavior: [spec.md](spec.md). Endpoint safety: [service-safety](../../platforms/service-safety.md).

Source: `FortniteFestivalWeb/src/pages/songinfo/components/chart/ScoreHistoryChart.tsx` (+ `components/common/GraphCard.tsx`, `hooks/chart/useChartData.ts`). Service: `FSTService/Api/PlayerEndpoints.cs:733-790` (`/api/player/{accountId}/history`), a pure keyless `GET` of a precomputed cache.

- **On the song page, not a separate page (operator batch 6.39, 2026-09-28).** `SongScoreHistorySection` (`Features/SongDetail/SongScoreHistorySection.swift`) sits after Intensity on Song Detail when a player is selected and has history for the song. One read per song on every instrument: `FestivalAPI.songHistory(accountId:songId:)` (`FestivalCore/FestivalAPI+SongHistory.swift`, the same endpoint without `instrument`; 404 unregistered, 202 syncing). Anything but available rows hides the section, like the web.
- Section: header "Score History" / "Select a bar to see more score details." above one glass card holding a **required** `InstrumentSelector` (graph look, shown even for one instrument, web `showSingleInstrumentSelector`) over the visible instruments with history (`SongScoreHistoryModel.instruments`, preferred → Lead → first), then the chart: accuracy bars on a trailing 0–100% axis (web `accuracyColor`, gold for a 100% full combo), the score line (`#4C7DFF`) on the leading axis, the web legend, and a pager. Points keep their series index as x and the x domain is the visible window, so paging slides the window without re-laying out points (operator batch 7.11). Tapping a bar shows its row under the chart. Below the card: the best five scores as separate rows (best first; the best purple and bold), then a purple "View all scores" (more than five) that expands every score in place ("Show top scores" collapses).
- **Instrument switch (issue #31, operator request; the web swaps instantly).** The selector updates at once; the chart and the rows below the card fade out together (easeIn 0.15 s), the new instrument swaps in with animations disabled, then fades in (easeOut 0.25 s) — `ScoreHistorySwap.plan` (Core). History for every instrument is already loaded, so "data loaded" is the next frame. A newer choice cancels a running swap; returning to the shown instrument mid-fade just fades back in. Reduce Motion (system or the app's `fst.accessibility.reduceMotion`) swaps instantly (HIG Accessibility: reduce automatic motion). The card keeps its size: the section measures the chart width once and passes it in (no first-frame pager pop), the pager's space is reserved (hidden, not in VoiceOver) for every instrument when any selectable instrument pages (`SongScoreHistoryModel.reservesPager`), and the card's height is pinned for the swap and released with an ease after it (only shrinks if a bar's detail row was open). The chart's accessibility label names the new instrument.
- **Season only on wide pages (issue #32):** the web list rows show a `SeasonPill` ("S40") before the score only when the viewport is at least 520 px (`QUERY_SHOW_SEASON`, `packages/theme/src/breakpoints.ts`); the tapped bar's detail card always shows it. iOS measures the Song Detail scroll view width (`onGeometryChange`, `viewportWidth`) and asks `ScoreRowSeasonPolicy` (Core, `.historyList` / `.historyDetail`, breakpoint 520 pt = CSS px). The iPhone app is portrait-only (≤440 pt), so its list rows never show the season and the "Season N" caption under the date is gone; only the detail row does. `ScoreSeasonPill` (`Features/SongLeaderboard/ScoreSeasonPill.swift`): "S9" in a 44×24 pt `@ScaledMetric` capsule, colours inverted for the catalogue's current season (`FestivalSession.catalogCurrentSeason`); accessibility text sizes drop the fixed frame, read "Season 9" and stack it under the date. The row label stays one line at standard sizes (web `truncate`; `minimumScaleFactor(0.75)`). VoiceOver reads "date, season N" (or "current season N"), score…. The Duo pane (`SongDetailDualSource`) passes no season. Open gaps: the web's difficulty pill (shown with the season) and its accuracy gate (hidden below 420 px) are not ported.
- **Deep link:** `AppRoute.playerHistory(song, instrument)` (`/songs/:id/:instrument/history`) still resolves, but `PlayerHistoryScreen` now renders `SongDetailScreen(historyFocus: instrument)`: the song page opens scrolled to Score History on that instrument with every score listed. The former list page, its sort sheet and line chart were removed.
- Invalid-score filtering (`useScoreFilter`) is not applied to history rows natively.
- IDs: `fst.song-detail.history` (section), `fst.song-detail.history.instrument.<Instrument|previous|next|centre>`, `fst.song-detail.history.chart`, `fst.song-detail.history.detail`, `fst.song-detail.history.row.<n>`, `fst.song-detail.history.view-all`.
