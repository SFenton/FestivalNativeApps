# Player profile (`/player/:accountId`) — iPhone notes

> **What:** iPhone implementation state and decisions for the player-profile page, both viewed (someone else, or no selection) and selected ("this is me"). **Read when:** changing `Features/Profile/PlayerProfileContent.swift`, `PlayerProfileScreen.swift` or `Features/Statistics/StatisticsScreen.swift`. Behavior: [spec.md](spec.md) (currently a stub — this file is the source of truth for what's actually built until spec.md is promoted). Selection/session mechanics: [profile-selection/ios.md](../../controls/profile-selection/ios.md).

Source: `FortniteFestivalWeb/src/pages/leaderboard/player/components/PlayerContent.tsx`, `.../pages/player/PlayerPage.tsx`, `.../pages/player/sections/OverallSummarySection.tsx`, `.../sections/InstrumentStatsSection.tsx`, `.../components/player/StatBox.tsx`. `App.tsx:95-105` also points the `/statistics` route at `PlayerPage` for the tracked/selected account. Service: `FSTService/Api/PlayerEndpoints.cs:14-83,190-259` (`GET /api/player/{accountId}`, side-effect-free per `.agents/controls/profile-selection/spec.md`).

## Implemented

- One `PlayerProfileContent` view is shared by two routes: the pushed `AppRoute.player(accountId:displayName:)` (`PlayerProfileScreen`) and the Statistics tab root (`StatisticsScreen`, only reachable once a player is selected — `FestivalTabPolicy.sections(profile:)`). Both use the identical Overview section, per-instrument sections and states; Statistics always renders in the selected (Deselect) state (its accountId is `session.selectedPlayer!.accountId`), with a defensive "no profile" `ContentUnavailableView` for the brief window between an explicit deselect and the tab bar hiding Statistics.
- Read: only `FestivalSession.viewPlayer(accountId:)` → the existing keyless, publication-aware `GET /api/player/{accountId}` (`FestivalAPI.playerProfile(accountId:)`, `FestivalCore/PlayerProfile.swift`). No selected-profile header, no tracking POST.
- **No name card (issue #97, 2026-10-03):** the large navigation title alone names the player (server `displayName` when available, else the route's or search result's); the former avatar-and-name glass card repeated it and pushed content down, so Global Statistics is now the first item under the title on iPhone, Duo, iPad and Mac. HIG: `apple-hig/references/hig/designing-for-ios.md` "Limit onscreen controls to keep primary content and tasks prominent"; `apple-hig/references/hig/layout.md` "Place important content toward the top/leading side in reading order." VoiceOver reaches the page through the title and the `isHeader` section headers. **No selection subtitle or badge:** the former native "This Is Me" / "Public Profile" line and seal had no web equivalent and were removed (operator, 2026-09-28); selection state shows only through the Select/Switch/Deselect action. Identity action is Select/Switch (viewed, response-proven, publication matches; nothing when already selected), or an honest "unverified"/"changed" notice (headerless or stale response; `PlayerProfileIdentityNotice`). That notice and any Select/Switch error share one glass card that is drawn only while either applies, first in the list and first in the fade-in stagger; otherwise the stagger starts at Global Statistics. The action is **not** on the page: it is a labelled toolbar item (`ProfileIdentityToolbarItem`; the tab-bar accessory `ProfileIdentityAccessory` was removed with the dock in issue #92) that the Duo puts in its rail ([nav-accessories.md](../../design/apple/nav-accessories.md)) — same rule `ProfileSelectionSheet` already used for its old in-sheet preview (`payload.publicationId == session.publicationId`).
- **Layout (operator batches 6.18/6.26/7.10, Lane AP5):** the web's flat item list. No card ever wraps other cards: every stat is its own glass card (`StatTileSurface`), headers sit above. Order: identity-notice card (only while paused or after a failed Select/Switch); "Global Statistics" header + five stat cards (Songs Played, Full Combos, Gold Stars, Avg Accuracy, Best Rank, computed **client-side** from the compact `scores` across Settings-visible instruments); "Instrument Statistics" header with the web description; then per instrument: `InstrumentSectionHeader`, empty-state card or — web `buildInstrumentStatsItems` order — the **Rank History** card, the stat cards, and the **Percentiles table** card.
- **Loading (operator batch 6.41):** a spinner until the profile read and, in parallel, every played visible instrument's rankings row and rank history (`ProfileExtrasLoader`) settle; then the page fades in and its sections stagger. `InstrumentStatsCard(preloaded:)` / `PlayerRankHistoryCard(preloaded:)` adopt those reads (failures stay on their own card with Retry) and still reload on Retry or a new publication.
- **Identity action:** Select / Switch only, as a prominent blue header button. **Deselect is not on the player page** (operator batch 7.10): it lives in the drawer (Lane A3). The red `btnDanger` tint for Deselect (batch 6.18) therefore applies to the drawer's button; `ProfileIdentityAction.isDestructive` keeps the colour rule.
- One glass section per Settings-visible instrument (`fst.settings.show*`, read directly via `@AppStorage`, matching `FestivalRootView.visibleInstruments` — no new init parameter, no edit to the Shell). The instrument header (`InstrumentSectionHeader`, web `InstrumentHeader` MD: 48 pt icon, bold title3) sits **above** the card, never inside it (operator, 2026-09-28). Tiles follow the web `InstrumentStatsSection` order: Songs Played, Full Combos (only when non-zero, like the web's FCs card), Gold / 5 / 4 / 3 / 2 / 1 Stars (non-zero only, `PlayerStarBreakdown`), Avg Accuracy, **Avg Stars** (web `formatClamped2`, or five gold `StarRating` stars at a 6.0 average), Best Rank, then the three global-rank tiles (below); or an empty-state footnote when that instrument has no scores.
- **Stat grid (Lane AP3, 2026-09-28):** `PlayerStatGrid` (`PlayerStatGrid.swift`) lays tiles out with a custom `Layout`: two columns on iPhone, three or four as the card widens (`StatGridColumns`: 140 pt minimum tile, 8 pt spacing, clamped `2...4`), each row as tall as its tallest tile. Not a `LazyVGrid`: the custom layout has its final columns and row heights on the first frame. Each tile has a flat 5% white fill (never glass on glass). A **linked tile is a button with an in-tile trailing chevron** (web `StatBox` chevron; `FestivalText.deemphasized`), press feedback 0.985 scale; a plain tile has no chevron and is one static accessibility element. Tiles are labelled in Title Case for VoiceOver (the caption is only drawn uppercase), with a hint naming the destination.
- **Stat links** (`FestivalCore/PlayerStatLinks.swift`, web `StatBox.onClick`):

  | Tile | Web target | iPhone |
  |---|---|---|
  | Overview Songs Played / Full Combos | `/songs`, `defaultSongFilters` + `hasScores`/`hasFCs` on every visible chart, Title | Songs tab, same preset (`SongsFilterPreset.overall`) |
  | Instrument Songs Played / Full Combos | `/songs`, that instrument, `cleanFilters` + one check, Score sort | Songs tab, same preset (`.instrument`), **Title** sort (native Songs has no Score sort) |
  | Overview / instrument Best Rank | `/songs/:id?instrument=` | `AppRoute.songDetail` (song resolved from the cached catalogue; no instrument focus) |
  | Global Rank (web Total Score Rank) | `/leaderboards/all?instrument=&rankBy=totalscore&page=` | `AppRoute.fullRankings(instrument:rankBy:"totalscore")` (first page) |
  | Gold / 5…1 Stars | Songs stars filter + Stars sort | Songs tab, `SongsFilterPreset.stars` |
  | Percentile table rows | Songs percentile bucket filter | Songs tab, `SongsFilterPreset.percentileBucket` (row button with chevron, `fst.player.percentile-row.<Instrument>.<N>`) |
  | Percentile tiles | Songs percentile sort | not shown (the Percentile tile is the rankings-derived field placement) |

  Songs presets go through `PlayerStatNavigator` (`\.playerStatNavigator`, installed by `FestivalRootView`): it writes the Songs tab's saved state (`SongsPresetStore`: `fst.songs.sortMode`, `fst.songs.sortAscending`, Shop filters, `SongPlayerScoreFilter.storageKey`), sets the root `songsInstrument`, clears the Songs search, pops Songs to its list and selects the tab (web `saveSongSettings` + `setQuery('')` + `navigate('/songs')`). The same navigator pushes Song Detail / Full Rankings on the current tab.
- **Select first (web `withProfileSwitch`):** on a viewed player's page every link first selects that player — immediately with no selection, after the existing Switch confirmation when another player is selected (the pending link then runs). While selection is paused (unverified or changed publication) Songs tiles are drawn plain (they would filter someone else's scores); Song Detail and Full Rankings still open. Android's `PlayerTileAction` follows the same rule.
- Sections fade in on load, staggered 125 ms (`festivalFadeIn(isLoaded:index:)`).
- Bands link (`NavigationLink` to `AppRoute.playerBands(accountId:displayName:)`, now owned by the Bands lane — `PlayerBandsScreen.swift` itself is untouched).
- Loading (`ProgressView`), syncing (202, reusing `ServiceUnavailableView` with manual Retry) and error (reusing `ServiceUnavailableView`, manual Retry) states, keyed on `accountId` + a retry counter + `session.publicationRevision` so a generation rollover re-fetches automatically.
- **Global rank** per instrument (`InstrumentStatsCard`, own loading/unranked/error/available states, independent of the profile load above it; three tiles — Global Rank, Total Score, Percentile — in the instrument's grid from the first frame: redacted placeholders while loading, "Unranked"/em dashes otherwise, plus a Retry line only on failure): reads `GET /api/rankings/{instrument}/{accountId}` (`FestivalCore/PlayerInstrumentRanking.swift`, `FestivalSession.playerInstrumentRanking(instrument:accountId:)`) — verified a pure read (`FSTService/Api/RankingsEndpoints.cs:272-327` only calls `db.GetAccountRanking`/`GetRankedAccountCount`, in-memory lookups against the precomputed board). This is the same fallback the web calls from `PlayerPage.tsx:145-154` (`api.getPlayerRanking`) when its player-stats read hasn't embedded canonical ranks yet; this app never calls player-stats at all, so every instrument always uses this fallback. Shows Total Score Rank (web `DEFAULT_METRICS`; the other four metrics are `EXPERIMENTAL_METRICS`, gated behind the not-yet-ported "Experimental Ranks" Settings toggle), its rating and a rank/field-size percentile (gold above the top 5%). HTTP 404 ("Account not found in rankings for this instrument") is an honest **unranked** state, not an error.
- **Live quirk (probed 2026-09-28):** unlike the list endpoint's `MapAccountRanking` helper, this single-account route's own inline response never backfills a blank `instrument` field from the request — production returns `"instrument":""`. `PlayerInstrumentRanking.validate(instrument:accountId:)` accepts an empty field and only rejects a populated-but-different one.
- **Quick Links** (`global` "Global Statistics", `instrument:<rawValue>` per visible instrument, `bands` "Bands"): eager `VStack`, so sections are tagged with `.quickLinkSection` and discovered in tree order (no explicit `sections:` list). `top-songs` from the spec table is not built — this page has no Top Songs section to link to (see Simplified below) — so it is left out rather than pointing at nothing. `PlayerProfileContent` owns the `QuickLinksController` and always adds `QuickLinksToolbarItem`; a new `showsRootTrailingItems` flag (default `false`) makes `StatisticsScreen` end that same `.toolbar` with `FestivalRootTrailingItems` and report `FestivalRootTrailingProvidedKey` itself, per the toolbar-order rule — `PlayerProfileScreen` (pushed) leaves it `false`.

## Graphs (Swift Charts)

`Features/Profile/PlayerProfileCharts.swift`: each played, Settings-visible instrument's stats card is followed by up to two glass chart cards. Charts are never nested inside the instrument's glass.

| Card | Web source | Data | Shown when |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Rank History** (`fst.player.rank-history.<Instrument>`) | `RankHistoryChart` (`pages/leaderboards/components/RankHistoryChart.tsx`, metric `totalscore`, 30 days) | `GET /api/rankings/{instrument}/{accountId}/history` (`FestivalCore/PlayerRankHistory.swift`); pure read, see [service-safety](../../platforms/service-safety.md) | at least one ranked snapshot; a failed read shows an inline retry card |
| **Percentiles** (`fst.player.percentiles.<Instrument>`) | percentile table (`PlayerPercentileTable.tsx`, `playerStats.ts:54-79`) as the web's table (`PlayerPercentileTableCard`: "Percentile / Songs" header, `PercentilePill` rows — gold outline ≤ 5%, sheared gold ≤ 1%); the Duo carousel alone keeps the bar chart | `PlayerProfileResponse.percentileBuckets(_:)`: `rank / totalEntries` from the compact scores; no extra request | at least one placed score |

- **Rank History layout (2026-09-28, Lane AP2):** one combined chart like the web: Total Score bars (leading axis, compact `K/M/B` ticks, each bar coloured by its rank's field placement via web `rankColor`, 80% opacity, 4 pt corners) and the rank line (trailing reversed axis `#N`, `#4C7DFF`, 2 pt, dots), rotated "Total Score"/"Rank" axis titles, the web legend (gradient swatch, line-and-dot) and `M/D/YY` dates, under a "#rank of N" summary. Swift Charts has one y scale, so ranks are projected into the value range (`RankHistoryChartScale`: web `getRankHistoryDomain` padding, best rank at the top). **Paging:** a window of `RankHistoryPaging.pageSize` bars (web `MIN_BAR_WIDTH` 96 / `BAR_GAP` 8 over the plot width) opens on the newest snapshots; a horizontal swipe (50 pt, web `SWIPE_THRESHOLD`) moves a page, and the web `GraphCard` buttons (Back one page / entry, Forward one entry / page, ids `fst.player.rank-history.<Instrument>.{back-page,back-entry,forward-entry,forward-page}`) plus a VoiceOver adjustable action on the chart move through history. Every snapshot is plotted at its series index and the x domain is the visible window (`chartXScale`, clipped plot), so paging slides the window instead of re-laying out and re-animating the line (operator batch 7.11). **Axis (batch 6.25, checked live on SFentonX):** the rank axis is reversed like the web (best rank at the top, padded `getRankHistoryDomain`), so a player ranked #4 throughout sees #4 near the top between #3 and #5 — not a bug. `chartScrollableAxes` was tried and dropped: scrollable charts paint nothing in one-pass renderers (hosted tests) and the window is simpler to page exactly. Snapshot days are calendar labels anchored at local midnight, since UTC midnight displayed a day early.
- **Date axis (#314):** each `M/D/YY` date is centred under its bar on every page through the shared `barChartDateAxis` ([chart-date-axis](../../patterns/chart-date-axis.md) R1); Swift Charts' default numeric axis put the label's leading edge at the bar centre. HIG Charts (`charts.md`, consider): "Anchor an unassociated-looking label to its grid line with a tick."
- **Accessibility and motion:** each chart has an `accessibilityChartDescriptor` (Audio Graph; Rank History's `RankHistoryDescriptor` carries both series and a separate rank axis) and summarizes the trend ("up N places"), and data-change animation is off when system *or* app Reduce Motion is on (`ChartMotion`).
- **Quick Links:** depth-1 "Rank History"/"Percentiles" entries under their instrument. The visible titles are short because longer ones wrap in the system menu. VoiceOver hears `spokenTitle` ("Lead Rank History") via `QuickLinkSection.accessibilityTitle`. Both sit inside the instrument's section and follow it in the menu (fixed in #11; see [quick-links/ios.md](../../controls/quick-links/ios.md)). `QuickLinksOrderJourneyTests` checks the order on Player and Statistics.
- **Gaps:** there is no metric picker (Total Score only, the web's non-experimental default), no "last 5 snapshots" list, and no tap-to-inspect bar (the web's `PressableChartPath`). Family/scope charts would need player-stats (blocked).

## Client-side stats, not the player-stats GET

`PlayerOverallStats`/`PlayerInstrumentStats` (`FestivalCore/PlayerProfile.swift`) mirror the web's `computeOverallStats`/`computeInstrumentStats` (`pages/player/helpers/playerStats.ts`) exactly enough for songs played, full-combo count/percent (floored to one decimal, matching the source), gold-star count (`stars == 6`, since the wire's validated range is already 0...6), average accuracy and best rank — computed **only** from the same compact `scores` array the Songs tab already decodes. `.agents/controls/profile-selection/spec.md` documents the player-stats GET as **not unconditionally read-only** (it can compute and store missing tiers), so this page never calls it. Per-instrument global rank is native via the rankings-board fallback above, and the percentile distribution and rank history are native graphs (see Graphs).

`PlayerScore.accuracy`/`PlayerValidScoreVariant.accuracy` already decode into the exact scale `ScoreFormatting.accuracy(_:)`/`accuracyTint(_:)` expect (compact wire `acc` × 1,000 = the leaderboard's ten-thousandths-of-a-percent unit) — no extra rescale needed; an earlier draft of this page added one, which was wrong and has been removed.

## Push animation (Lane AP3, 2026-09-28)

Reproduced on iPhone 17 Pro (iOS 26.5) from a leaderboard row, search and the Statistics tab, fixture and live. Causes in Profile, all fixed:

- **Toolbar re-layout mid-push:** the Select/Switch/Deselect button was only added once the read proved it, so the Liquid Glass bar morphed its trailing group twice during and just after the push. The planned action (`plannedIdentity`, from session state) is now in the bar from the first frame, disabled (`fst.player.select.pending`) until proven or while paused.
- **Tile identity churn:** `StatTile.id` was a fresh `UUID()` per body pass, so every re-render removed and re-inserted every tile (cross-fades mid-push). Ids are now stable keys.
- **Late sections without reserved height:** the global-rank spinner row grew into a tile row, and the Rank History card went from nothing to a ~360 pt chart, pushing every card below down. Rank tiles are placeholders in the grid; the Rank History card shows `RankHistoryPlaceholder` (same summary/plot/legend/pager heights) while loading.
- **Chart first-frame resize:** `RankHistoryCharts` started at width 0 (every snapshot on one page, no pager), then re-paged with the data-change animation and grew a pager row. It is now seeded with the card width measured on the placeholder (`chartWidth(forCardWidth:)`), and width changes never animate.
- **Main-thread stall as the push lands:** the page is an eager `VStack` (Quick Links needs tree order), so every instrument's Swift Charts (up to nine percentile charts at once, then each rank-history chart as it arrived) were built with the page; on a live profile with every instrument played (SFentonX) the page stayed blank for most of a second after the spinner, then faded in. Charts now sit in `NearViewport` (built within ~1,700 pt of the screen, one-way latch) behind exact-height placeholders.
- **Sheet dismissal racing the push (search):** `ProfileSelectionSheet` dismissed and pushed at once, so two transitions ran together and the pushed page's Back/actions appeared only after both. It now places the route without a push animation under the sheet and lets the dismissal reveal it. (Global search's sheet, Lane A2, still pushes during its dismissal.)
- **Reload on reappear:** the Rank History card reloaded (collapsing to nothing) on every Back to the page; it now keeps a `loadedKey` like the profile and rank reads.

Shared (not Profile): during the push the iPhone floating Quick Links dock briefly shows the outgoing page's and the incoming page's buttons side by side, and the pushed page's global Search button arrives a beat after the push (Lane A2/AM).

## Simplified vs. web this pass

No family/global statistics cards (`OverallSummarySection`'s `pad`/`pro_strings`/`pro_drums` scopes — needs per-instrument ranks on a *scope*, not one instrument), no Top Songs section (so no `top-songs` quick link either), no band/member card, no first-run carousel, no experimental-ranks metric picker (Total Score only, matching the web's un-experimental default). Star-count and percentile tiles stay plain until native Songs has a stars filter, a percentile filter and Score/Stars/Percentile sorts (Songs lane); Song Detail opens without the web's `?instrument=` focus/auto-scroll (`AppRoute.songDetail` has no instrument); Full Rankings opens on page 1, not the page containing the rank.
