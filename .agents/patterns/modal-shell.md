# Modal shell

> **What:** the shared sheet, dialog and alert container with its title, dismissal and standard Close affordance. **Read when:** presenting a scoped task, confirmation or notice.

Status: **current**, 2026-10-10. Provenance: #23, #24, #25, #94, #96, #125, #139, #140, #183, #360, #373, #400, #565, #586, #607, #612, #638.

## Intent

A modal identifies one short, scoped task, exposes its platform-standard dismissal and closes before another modal opens. Features own body content and actions; the shared shell owns chrome, focus/dismissal behavior and the header.

## Web source (behavior reference)

| Web | Behavior |
|---|---|
| `FortniteFestivalWeb/src/components/modals/components/ModalShell.tsx` (`ModalShell`, `DEFAULT_TRANSITION_MS`, `FOCUSABLE_SELECTOR`) | One portal shell owns overlay, Escape, focus containment, focus restoration and nested-layer inertness. |
| `FortniteFestivalWeb/src/components/modals/Modal.tsx` (`Modal`) | Variant content supplies reset/apply behavior inside the common shell. |

## Rules

- **R1. Use only the shared shell.** Every feature modal uses its platform canonical component; never construct an unowned sheet, dialog or `ContentDialog`.
- **R2. Name the task.** The common header/dialog title is concise and announced before body content. Apple HIG Modality: “Identify the task with a title or explanatory/guidance text so people can regain their place after switching context.” Apple evidence (#429): the first-run guide journey (`FirstRunJourneyTests.testGuideTitleIsReadFirstAtLargestText`) asserts, over the whole app snapshot at default and AX5 sizes, title → Close → body, with only the system "Sheet Grabber" (sheet chrome UIKit places first) outside the sheet's content; the system inline title grows about 1.22× at AX5 (UIKit's bar cap, `system-bar-title-size`), so modal-title journeys assert growth ≥ 1.15×, not the 1.35× page-text rule. The sheet-header journey `ModalHeaderAccessibilityJourneyTests` (#447) asserts the same title → Close → body sequence for Notifications and Paths at rest and with content scrolled under the header (`scroll-edge`), and that their body text (a row, an activation card) grows > 1.35× and reads back whole. **Owner-approved variant (#360, Paths sheet on folded iPhone Duo only):** where `DeviceLayout.pose == .folded`, the Paths title names the selected instrument, "Paths · <instrument>" (updating as it changes), and its instrument selector shows only the instrument icon (owner: "On Duo folded, don’t show instrument name in paths modal selector- icon is fine. Title can say Paths {dot} {instrument name}"; HIG Designing for iPhone Duo: "prefer a symbol wherever one works"). The selector keeps its VoiceOver label and value ("Instrument, <name>") and its icon scales with Dynamic Type. Every other pose and platform (unfolded/book Duo, iPhone, iPad, Mac) and every other modal keep the plain task title; do not revert or spread it without an owner ask. `SongPathsSheet.title(for:pose:)` / `showsInstrumentName(pose:)` own it. **Owner-approved variant (#612, Apple Paths selector row only):** where the row is too narrow for icon + name (a Paths sheet whose window is no wider than the web's `MOBILE_BREAKPOINT`, 768 pt: iPhone in portrait, a compact iPad window; folded Duo at every size), the Instrument and View menus show only their icon (instrument artwork; View `photo` / `doc.text` SF Symbols, scaled with Dynamic Type) plus the chevron, and Difficulty fills the rest of the row, like the web's mobile `PathsModal` (owner: "On devices where icon and string won't fit neatly in paths modal, just show instrument icon and image/text icon in dropdowns like web"). Each keeps its VoiceOver label and value ("Instrument, <name>", "View, <mode>") and a 44 pt target; every View item carries its symbol (HIG Menus: "icons for all or none", should). The accessibility-size stacked layout, wider sheets (a large iPhone in landscape) and the full-window viewer (R12) keep icon + name; the title stays "Paths" outside folded Duo. Never use a text-vanishing `ViewThatFits` fallback (it failed the XCUITest Dynamic Type audit). `SongPathsPolicy.selectorsShowNames(_:stacked:)` owns it; other modals are unaffected. Evidence: `SongPathsSelectorTests`, `SongPathsPolicyTests` (rules) and `DuoPathsSelectorJourneyTests` (rendered: folded and iPhone icon-only selectors measured in pixels, title following an instrument switch, "Instrument, <name>" / "View, <mode>" kept, unfold/refold, iPhone row order and widths, stacked large-text names with a Dynamic Type/clipping/hit-region audit).
- **R3. Offer the native dismissal.** Apple sheets use the system Close and permit vertical dismissal where no input is at risk; Android uses the Material Close button plus back/scrim/swipe; Windows uses the standard `ContentDialog` Close command and Esc/light dismiss. Material's scrim stays a "Close sheet" TalkBack stop, but above a fully expanded compact sheet only its 8 dp `SHEET_TOP_GAP_DP` strip is uncovered. The 48 dp header Close and Back are its equivalent, so ATF journeys ignore that one touch-target finding rather than drop the scrim (agent decision #418; the owner may override). Apple HIG Modality: “Always provide an obvious, platform-conventional dismissal.” Windows evidence (#400): `tools/windows/journeys/a11y-modals.json` has one `a11y_matrix` page per `FestivalDialog` caller. Each page asserts the title is read first, then the body, then the commands with Close last (R2). It first waits for the title and body text to be on screen, then checks Close's Narrator phrase, asserts every Close and alert command is at least 40×40 epx with hit-test probes 18.5 epx from its centre on each side, checks that Tab reaches Close, and checks that Esc closes and restores focus. Every dialog command is 40 epx tall: `DialogChrome.CommandTargets` raises the template's Primary/Secondary/Close buttons to `FSTMinTargetSize` (see the agent decision below). Buttons a dialog builds in its content use the same `DialogChrome.TargetSize`: the feedback form's Attach Media (a literal 40 before) and its discard bar's Discard and Keep Editing (31.7 epx until #433) (`ModalMarkupTests.FeedbackContentButtons_KeepTheMinimumTouchTarget`; `a11y-feedback.json` asserts 40×40 epx). The `windows-ui` workflow (`.github/workflows/windows-ui.yml` → `tools/windows/ui_ci.py`) builds the app and runs the journey with `--scan --tabs 30` at default text (compact and medium) and at 225% text (compact). `tools/windows/tests/test_modal_journeys.py` fails when a new caller has no page or the journey leaves the `windows-ui` runs. Under contrast themes, `DialogChrome.CommandLabelsWithoutBackplate` (#239) keeps WinUI's Window-coloured text backplate off the dialog commands and the First Run pips. `journeys/a11y-modal-legibility.json` (#518) checks this with pixel probes under Desert and Night sky, and checks that the Feedback field hints wrap at 225% text.
- **R4. Keep Close out of feature bodies.** Do not draw a custom xmark, footer Close or live-apply Done control. Apple’s system `FestivalSheetCloseItem`, Android’s 48 dp `FestivalModalCloseButton` and Windows’ command row are the only shared close affordances.
- **R5. Keep the body below the header.** The Apple shell applies the shared top fade (#94); Android and Windows retain a hard content edge. Modal presentation never covers its own title or Close control, and a sheet never grows past its window: on the Mac, `macSheetFrame` opens a sheet at its ideal height but lets it shrink to `MacSheetMetrics.shortestHeight` (400 pt) when the screen is shorter (a small display, a "Larger Text" display scaling, or a CI runner's ~676 pt visible screen). AppKit fits the sheet window to the screen, and a minimum equal to the ideal height overflowed both edges, pushing the title and the bottom bar's Close outside the sheet (agent decision #462; HIG Sheets › macOS "Present a sheet in a reasonable default size" keeps the default; the owner may override). An explicit `minHeight` (Search's 520) is the caller's floor. Evidence: `notificationsSheetKeepsItsTitleAndCloseOnAShortScreen` (a sheet taller than the screen keeps its title in the header, Close in the bottom bar, both read first and last; fails without the floor). On Windows, `ContentDialog`'s template `ContentScrollViewer` never scrolls vertically (`VerticalScrollBarVisibility="Disabled"`, WindowsAppSDK `generic.xaml`), so content past the window's edge is clipped. A body that can outgrow the window at large text or in a short window brings its own `ScrollViewer`, and the title and command row stay fixed: Feedback (`FitScroller`), What's New, Privacy, Paths and the first-run carousel (`fst.first-run.body`, #420: at 200–225% text in the compact window the pips and the end of the description were cut off, and Tab focused an unseen pip). Never restyle or reach into the template's `ContentScrollViewer` instead; see the agent decision below. `ModalMarkupTests` and the `modal-shell/windows-template-scroller` guard hold it. On Android the body's touch and TalkBack bounds also stop at that edge: every sheet and dialog body (and the feedback form's) sits in `FestivalModalBody`, so a row or field scrolled under the header never takes part of Submit, Reset or Close. Compose 1.9 extends a scroll-clipped node's touch bounds up to 24 dp past the viewport, and the body, later in the tree, wins the overlap: CI's 200% text feedback form cut Submit's TalkBack target to 47 dp (agent decision #422 below; the owner may override). Evidence: `FestivalModalTest#bodyKeepsScrolledRowsTouchBoundsOffTheHeader` (with a control that fails once Compose clips touch bounds itself) and the `@DeviceCi` connected `FeedbackFormJourneyTest#headerActionsKeepFullTouchAreaOverAFieldScrolledUnderAtLargeText` (Submit and Close publish ≥ 48 dp with a field scrolled under them; reading order title → Submit → Close). A row scrolled fully out of the body is therefore not visible to TalkBack: a journey scrolls each row into view before reading its platform node (`SongsFilterAccessibilityJourneyTest` read Year 2010 after scrolling to 2020 and failed CI on phones).
- **R6. Preserve the feedback-form exception.** It has potentially lossy text input, so it uses Cancel and Submit with discard confirmation rather than the ordinary Close-only task shell. Apple HIG Sheets: “Single-view sheets: Cancel on the top toolbar's leading edge; Done, when present, trailing.”
- **R11. Take feedback media beside the form, not over it (Apple, #373).** Photos, videos and image/movie files dropped anywhere on the feedback form attach to it on every Apple surface (iPhone, iPad, Duo, Mac), through the same limits and location removal as a picked file, with an accent outline and "Drop to Attach" while something droppable is over it (none while sending). At regular width on iPad and the iPhone Duo inner display the form opens page-sized (`FestivalSheetSizing.regularPage`; a presented sheet keeps its opening size, so the room has to be there from the start) and Attach Media › Photo Library opens the system photo picker **inline** in a pane beside the form (title "Photo Library" and a "Hide" text button, never Done/Close). The form and the library are a two-up row of the canonical [hinge-columns](hinge-columns.md) `HingeRow` (`fillsHeight`), never a feature-local stack: on a partially folded Duo they end and start at the active fold's clearance (hinge-columns R1); flat, on iPad or on the flat Duo, they divide the sheet, the row's free space, at its midpoint (hinge-columns R7). Ticking a photo attaches it, unticking removes it, and removing its thumbnail unticks it. iPhone, compact windows and the Mac keep the form card and the presented picker; Choose File… always presents the system Files picker (no public inline form, on any platform). See the agent decision below. `FeedbackPhotoLibraryPlacement`, `FeedbackPickerLinks`, `FeedbackFormPanes` and (iOS drop, past the iOS 18+ `Form` drop interception) `FeedbackSheetDropInteraction` own it. Evidence: `FeedbackFormModelTests` (drop import, placement, links); `FeedbackFormPanesHostedTests` (book pose: form ends at the fold clearance, library starts after it, both full height; flat: halves of the sheet; hidden: form spans the sheet); `FeedbackMediaJourneyTests` (iPad and unfolded Duo: library beside the form, tick attaches, removing the thumbnail unticks, Hide keeps attachments, Cancel confirms; iPhone and the folded Duo: no pane, the picker is presented over the form; passing on iPad, iPhone 17 Pro and the folded Duo, while the unfolded Duo pose can't be set from this host's tooling, so `FeedbackFormPanesHostedTests` covers its geometry; iPad Split View drop from Photos onto the Description box shows the highlight, counted by the Debug marker `fst.settings.feedback.drop.shown`, and attaches).
- **R7. Serialize presentations.** Dismiss a sheet before opening another; a confirmation alert may sit above a modal, but never stack independent alerts. Apple HIG Sheets: “Display only one sheet at a time from the main interface.” **A finished task's notice follows its sheet (Apple, #565):** when the feedback form's report is filed (or received) the form closes itself, and its host presents the single-Done "Report Sent"/"Request Sent" alert from the sheet's `onDismiss`, never over the form (whose Cancel/Submit no longer mean anything) or over a sheet that is still dismissing. Done only dismisses the alert and `accessibilityFocusMove` returns VoiceOver focus to the row that opened the form. A failure ("Couldn't Send") or HTTP error is a confirmation-style alert on the form and keeps it open with its input. HIG Modality: “Let people dismiss a modal before presenting another”; HIG Sheets: a sheet presents “a scoped, context-related task people complete before returning to the parent view”. `FeedbackSentPresentation` owns the order. Evidence: `FeedbackSentPresentationTests` (alert only after dismissal; Cancel shows nothing) and the `FeedbackSentJourneyTests` XCUITest (no `submit`/`close`/field under the alert, Done only, accessibility audit, focus back on the row; failed and refused filings keep the form), which failed on the old order; its report, request and failure journeys run in `apple-ci`'s iPhone and iPad steps. Android: `FeedbackViewModel` moves the result to `sent` as it clears the form, and `FestivalAlertDialog` without `dismissLabel` is the single-action notice; evidence `FeedbackViewModelTest`, `FeedbackUiTest#backClosesTheResultAlert` (one covering modal) and the connected `FeedbackFormJourneyTest` (ATF; the alert reads title → message → Done, with no Close, Submit or Cancel).
- **R8. Keep surfaces off a separating hinge with one side rule (Android).** Sheets, centred dialogs and the full-page service status ([empty-error-states](empty-error-states.md) R7) never straddle a **separating** fold or hinge. Material 3: “Never place interactive content or critical information across the hinge area.” A vertical hinge (book posture) keeps the wider side of the surface's page, the leading side on a tie; a horizontal hinge (tabletop) always keeps the part **below** it, whichever half is larger, and only a page that ends inside the hinge keeps the part above. Flat folds keep the ordinary placement. The rule lives once in `core/nav/HingeSide.kt` (`HingeSide.keep` / `HingeSide.padding`, page-rectangle aware); `SheetHinge`, `DialogHinge` and `serviceStatusHingeSide` only adapt its result to sheet padding, a centring area or page padding. Agent decision (#140, design review of #212): one shared helper after the three surfaces' copies drifted on tabletop; the owner may override. Evidence: `HingeSideTest`, `SheetHingeTest` and `DialogHingeTest` in `android-unit`; the connected `SongPathsDeviceTest#halfOpenSheetStaysOnOneSideAndReadsInOrderAtEveryTextSize` (`@HalfOpenFoldJourney`, #384: ATF, reading order, no straddle at 100% and 200% text) runs in CI `android-fold` on a half-open book-fold emulator.
- **R9. Size centred dialogs from the window (Android; Windows window-sized dialogs follow it).** `FestivalModalDialog` always takes the window width less 16 dp margins, capped at 560 dp (Material 3: “Centered dialog (max 560dp wide)”), and never the platform's preferred dialog width (`usePlatformDefaultWidth`), which measured 320 dp in a 923 dp landscape phone window. First run (#139) fixed this for itself with a `compact` flag; #183 found What's New and Privacy Policy still 320 dp wide on landscape phones and moved the rule into the shell, removing the flag. Feature dialogs never set their own width. **Windows:** a `ContentDialog` sized from its window (Paths: `max(320, min(1200, window − 24))`) follows the window while it is open through `DialogChrome.FollowWindow`: the template reads `ContentDialogMaxWidth`/`MinWidth`/`MaxHeight` only when it is applied, so the dialog otherwise kept its opening size, clipped on the right in a narrowed window and never crossing its content's width breakpoint (#571). Evidence: `a11y-paths-swap.json` `paths-selectors-swap-*` (resize across Paths' 900 epx selector swap with the dialog open).
- **R10. Hold the page's decorative motion while a modal covers it (Android).** Every Festival modal registers with `ModalCoverage` through `CoversBackdrop` (the shell does it; the feedback form, the R6 exception, wraps its own `Dialog` in it) and gives its window one more `LocalModalDepth`. Content behind the newest modal reads `coveredByModal()` and holds a still frame: the shared backdrop (#83), the `FestivalMarqueeText` scroll (held at its start, semantics `Held`), the Songs/Shop pulse and breathe (their reduced-motion frame), the first-run demo pulse and the service-status pulse. The newest modal's own content keeps animating (the first-run demos run inside the tour; a confirmation alert holds the sheet beneath it). Material 3 modal dialogs and sheets “block interaction” and sit over a scrim, so the page's loops are unseen work: a clipped leaderboard name kept the Leaderboards page drawing every vsync (837 frames in 15 s) behind the first-run tour on the FST_Phone emulator. Each consumer has a Robolectric regression that opens a real Festival modal over it and checks the held frame and the resume (`FestivalModalTest`, `MarqueeTextTest`, `ShopMotionHoldTest`; the first-run and service-status pulses sample their drawn alpha through the internal `LocalMotionProbe`, `null` in the app, in `FirstRunDemoPulseHoldTest` and `ServiceStatusUiTest`), so a new loop that reads `coveredByModal()` adds one too. On device, the `@DeviceCi` journey `ArtworkBackgroundModalAccessibilityJourneyTest` (#436) opens each modal kind (first-run `FestivalModalDialog`, Privacy Policy `FestivalModalSheet`, the feedback form's own `Dialog`, the Reset `FestivalAlertDialog`) at font scale 1.0 and 2.0. With each one open it checks that the backdrop is `covered`, the open count is 1, the modal is the window TalkBack reads (title before Close or the alert buttons, which are labelled 48 dp buttons, in the order TalkBack follows: `publishTalkBackTree` after launch, at least one traversal link per walk) and no window exposes the backdrop. After close it checks `animated` and a count of 0. With animator scale 0, `reduced-motion` outranks `covered`. A new modal kind joins that journey. The held `FestivalMarqueeText` has its own `@DeviceCi` journey, `CoveredMarqueeAccessibilityJourneyTest` (#509). It runs on Song Details with a title that overflows the header. Under each first-run tour and the Paths sheet, the title's mode is `Held` and TalkBack reads the modal, not the covered title. On the page, TalkBack reads the title whole, once, as a heading. At font scale 2.0 it stays `Wrapped` and never `Held`. With animator scale 0 it stays `Truncated` with an ellipsis, under the sheet and after it closes. Agent decision (#186): Android holds every covered page loop, not just the backdrop #83 asked for; Apple and Windows keep their own #28/#83 scope until checked. The owner may override.
- **R12. Owner-approved variant (#368, Apple Paths only): cover the window where the image and text fit side by side.** On the iPhone Duo inner display (unfolded or book pose at regular width), iPad in the sidebar shell and the Mac, Paths opens over the whole window instead of a sheet; on iPhone, the folded Duo and a compact iPad it stays the R1 sheet. Owner: "Duo unfolded/partial fold/MacOS: can we have a full-screen modal (align with platform design) that can show image and text side-by-side? If partial fold, enforce side by side; if full unfold (or iPad/MacOS), allow option to see full-screen image, text, or side-by-side." HIG Modality: "Full-screen modals suit temporary media and multistep work" (consider). `SongPathsPolicy.coverage` chooses; `festivalModalPresentation` presents, and it is the only way a Festival modal covers the whole window:
  - iOS/iPadOS: `fullScreenCover` with the opaque card colour, the same `FestivalModal` title and Close (R2, R4). A cover has no system swipe, so R3's vertical dismissal is `PullDownToDismiss` on the viewer's scroll views (iOS only; disabled in sheets, where the system swipe already works) — the What's New cover's existing gesture.
  - macOS: there is no cover, so it is a sheet sized to the presenting window's content area (`MacWindowSizedSheet`: the sheet window is matched to the parent's `contentLayoutRect` once attached, because SwiftUI adds the sheet's bar outside a fitted frame), Close-only (HIG Modality: "macOS/tvOS use a button in the main content view"), with `presentationSizing(.fitted)` instead of `.page`.

Its View menu still follows the live layout (Side by Side appears or falls back to the Settings default view).
  - Contrast: What's New covers the iPhone through the same `festivalModalPresentation` but stays a centred sheet on the Duo inner display (`WhatsNewPresentationStyle`: "a cover stretched the iPhone page"; [whats-new](../controls/whats-new/ios.md)); only Paths, which shows two panes, covers it. Do not spread the cover to other modals without an owner ask. Evidence: `SongPathsPolicyTests`, `SongPathsSheetRenderTests` (book pose and wide Side by Side), `DuoPathsSelectorJourneyTests.testUnfoldedOffersSideBySideBookPoseSplitsAtHingeAndFoldingFallsBack`.

Dismissal is unchanged: Close, Back, a scrim tap and a swipe down (straight to dismissed, as every other sheet already did); R8's hinge side still applies. - **R15. No line under the header (Android, #607).** `FestivalModalHeader` (title row with Close) runs straight into the body: no `HorizontalDivider`, border or 1 dp rule between them, in any sheet or dialog. The header's own spacing and title separate it. Owner (#573 → #607): "Filter Suggestions has gray line at bottom of header … If not, standardize on no line". Filter Suggestions was the only modal that drew one (a `HorizontalDivider(color = BrandTokens.glassBorder)` as the first body item). Songs Sort/Filter, Shop Filter, Quick Links, Paths, Profiles, Find Rival, Notifications, What's New, Privacy, Licenses, History Sort, Feedback and the First Run dialog never did. Material 3 has no rule that a sheet header needs a divider: dividers are optional grouping aids ("Use dividers sparingly", m3.material.io Divider guidelines), and Compose `ModalBottomSheet` draws none of its own. Dividers *inside* a body stay allowed where they group content, such as What's New's line above its bottom Dismiss bar and Licenses' line between the metadata and the license text. Evidence: Robolectric `SuggestionsUiTest#filterSheetHasNoLineUnderItsHeader` (the form starts at the header row's bottom edge; it fails with the divider restored, 315 vs 312 px). The connected `ModalCloseJourneyTest` reads the composited screen under every sheet's and dialog's header and fails on a thin full-width band of a non-surface colour. Negative control: "a 1.1 dp line … spans the header" with the divider restored. The rule is enforced by tests, not a pattern guard: a divider is only wrong where it follows the header, which a single-line guard cannot see.
- **R16. Owner-approved variant (#634, the feedback form's busy state): a centred spinner, title and subtitle replace the fields while sending and filing.** Owner: "When we transition to uploading/filing, content in modal should fade out gracefully, arc spinner and title/subtitle should appear (“Uploading Media”, progress bar for subtitle, then “Filing Issue” or “Filing Request” with subtitle “We’re filing your issue/request. Please wait while we upload it.”) if transferring between media and filing, title/subtitle/bar should fade out then fade in new text, but spinner can remain. This should be centered horizontally and vertically in the modal." Scope: only the R6 feedback form, from Submit until it closes or fails; every other modal and page keeps the base rules (a loading spinner is one white, named busy indicator with no title or subtitle, [empty-error-states](empty-error-states.md) R6; one fade per swap, [load-transition](load-transition.md) R2/R6). The fields fade out, then the platform's canonical white arc spinner fades in with a title and subtitle, centred in the body under the header, which keeps the form's title, disabled Submit and Cancel/Close (R3, R6). With media the title is **Uploading Media** over a determinate bar that follows the upload; filing reads **Filing Issue**/**Filing Request** over the owner's sentence. On the hand-off the title, subtitle and bar fade out (150 ms, the web `QUICK_FADE_MS`, as in load-transition R10), then the new ones fade in; the spinner stays and is not restarted. Reduce Motion swaps without fades. Screen readers meet one named busy indicator, then the title (each new step's title is announced), then the bar with its value or the sentence; the hidden fields are out of the tree, and a failure returns them with every value kept. **Agent decision (Android, #634; Windows and Apple follow it):** a form without media skips the upload step and shows the Filing copy from Submit, because "Uploading Media" would be inaccurate and would flash for a fraction of a second; the owner may override with `/choose`. Android: `FeedbackDialog.kt` `FeedbackBusySwap`/`FeedbackBusyView`, progress from `MultipartFormBody`'s byte count; evidence Robolectric `FeedbackBusyViewTest` and `FeedbackUiTest`, device `FeedbackFormJourneyTest` (ATF and reading order in the upload step with media, including the bar's spoken percentage, and the filing step; 200 % text). |---|---|---|---|---|
