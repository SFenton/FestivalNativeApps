# View all call to action

> **What:** the full-width "View all" button that ends a card of preview rows and opens the full list on its own page (never expands the card in place, R8): View Full Leaderboard, View All Rivals, View All (Rival Detail categories), View All Rankings (N), View All Scores. **Read when:** adding a card that previews part of a list, or changing any of these buttons.

Status: **current**, 2026-10-08. Provenance: operator batch 6.29, #41, #68, #207, #264, #268, #321, #324, #352, #382.

## Intent

A card that previews the top of a longer list ends the same way on every page: one wide, brand-purple button after the rows that names where it goes. People learn it once and find it in the same place on Song Details, Rivals, Compete and Leaderboards.

The web places the button **after** the rows and only for non-empty, error-free previews (`InstrumentCard.tsx:300-327`).

## Rules

1. **R1. One button, after the rows.** The CTA sits below the card's last row (after the selected player's appended row, when shown), spans the card's width and centres a semibold label. It shows only when the card shows rows: never while loading, for an empty card or on a failed read (Apple Song Detail's instrument card keeps it on a failed read so the full chart stays reachable).
   - **Apple: inside the card (#382).** Where the rows share one `FestivalGlassSection` card ([surface-materials](surface-materials.md) R8), the CTA is the card's `action:` and ends the card inside it: 10 pt (`actionInset`) from the card's edges, so its 12 pt corners are concentric with the card's 22 pt, and a **flat, opaque** brand purple with no material of its own (`festivalCardAction`; surface-materials R6). It is never Liquid Glass. No hairline separates it from the last row. A preview made of separate tiles (Profile's Bands groups) keeps the CTA below them on the tinted material. [Agent decision below](#agent-decision-apple-view-all-inside-the-card-382).
2. **R2. Brand purple, not the web's frosted fill.** Owner decision (operator batch 6.29): the natives fill the CTA with the brand purple (`#7C3AED`) and white text instead of the web's frosted card. It is never the platform accent colour.
3. **R3. Platform minimum target, no per-consumer overrides.** Height is at least the platform target (Apple 44 pt, Android 48 dp, Windows 40 epx `FSTMinTargetSize`). Consumers set no margin, height, colour, corner or font of their own; the canonical component owns them.
4. **R4. Label first, then the card.** Labels are the web copy in Title Case (View Full Leaderboard, View All Rivals, View All Rankings (N), View All Scores, and plain View All where the card title already names the list). The copy is "View All", never "See All" (owner, #321). The accessible name starts with the visible label, then names the card or chart ("View Full Leaderboard, Lead"; WCAG 2.5.3 label in name), the role is Button and each card's CTA has its own test ID. On Apple, a card or pane container that carries its own identifier around the CTA sets `.accessibilityElement(children: .contain)` first; otherwise its identifier shadows the CTA's (Duo Rival Detail panes and Compete cards, #321).
5. **R5. Contrast and transparency modes keep it readable.** Windows contrast themes draw it HighlightText on Highlight with no automatic text backplate (`HighContrastAdjustment="None"`); Apple's in-card CTA is always an opaque purple (white ≈ 5.7:1); the below-tiles one turns opaque under Reduce Transparency and the in-app contrast/transparency toggles.
6. **R6. Not this pattern.** A section title's trailing "View All" link ([section-headers](section-headers.md) R8) and Search's "View All Results" are links, not this CTA. A preview card's own bottom "see the whole list" row is this CTA, never a separate in-card text row: Rival Detail's category cards end with it (owner, #321).
7. **R7. Bottom rows become the CTA; title-row links stay links.** **Agent decision (#321, 2026-10-06): option A; owner may override with `/choose`.** The owner asked that Rivals' in-card "See All" use the purple button and that every "See All" read "View All". Bottom-of-card "see the whole list" rows become this CTA (labelled "View All" plus the card in the spoken name). Title-row links keep the section-headers R8 link style with "View All" copy. Rationale: HIG Buttons "Keep to one or two prominent buttons per view" (should), the web's header links on Rival Detail and Profile Bands, and the existing R8 pattern. Option B, every link a purple button, was rejected. Windows check (#321): Windows Rival Detail ended each card with an in-card text link ("View All N Songs"), so it is a bottom row and became this CTA, as on Apple; the Windows Rivals hub header links stay links.

8. **R8. Open the full list; never expand the card in place.** Owner decision (#324: "We should go to a sub-page with sorts like web app"; supersedes operator batch 6.39's in-place Score History expansion). The CTA navigates to the full list's page (pushed, or the trailing pane in a split), and the card keeps its preview length. Where a split is possible the CTA opens its page in the trailing pane as the card page's sub-page and stays selected while it is open (Song Detail's View All Scores, #324; its instrument and Duos/Trios/Quads View full leaderboard, #342/#367; Leaderboards' View All Rankings, #352 agent decision A, owner may override with `/choose`; all through `ListDetailLink`). Song Detail's View All Scores opens Player History for the card's instrument (web `ScoreHistoryChart` → `PlayerHistoryPage`). The rule rests on the owner decision and the web and Android precedent (web `ScoreHistoryChart` → `PlayerHistoryPage`, Android `SongHistoryCard` → `PlayerHistoryScreen`), not on an Apple guideline: the HIG Lists and tables "limit the number of rows … a way to view more" advice is a watchOS platform consideration only and is not cited for iOS, iPadOS or macOS. On Apple the page is pushed with the native back button (or shown in the split's trailing pane). On Windows the CTA navigates the content `Frame` to `PlayerHistoryPage` (title-bar back returns, Fluent navigation basics), and Song Detail's card keeps only the chart and its top five rows, with no sort (#324).

### Agent decision: Apple View All inside the card (#382)

Agent decision (#382, 2026-10-08, owner may override with `/choose`). The owner asked: "would it also be platform-acceptable if we moved purple 'View All' buttons into those cards, and made them purple Liquid Glass? What would be best platform design here?" (after #381 put each section's rows in one card).

| Option | What you'd see | Guidance (strength) | Web / pattern precedent | Trade-offs |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A. Keep the purple button below the card | A tinted-material button under each group card: two surfaces per section. | HIG Buttons: "Leave enough space around a button to set it apart" (**should**). | The web's CTA is one more frosted card after its frosted row cards; with one native group card a detached button no longer reads as the list's last element. | No churn; the button looks unrelated to its card. |
| **B. Inside the card, full-width flat purple (chosen)** | The card ends with a solid purple button 10 pt from its edges, concentric 12 pt corners. | HIG Buttons: "use a prominent style (accent-color background) for the most likely action" (**should**). HIG Color: "For a primary action, color the background, not symbols or text" (**should**). HIG Lists and tables: grouped style "headers, footers, extra spacing to separate groups" (**should**). HIG Buttons (macOS): "Place them near the view, usually within or beneath it" (**should**). | Web `ViewFullLeaderboardCta`, `RankingCard` `viewAllButton`: the CTA is the row stack's last element on the rows' surface. [surface-materials](surface-materials.md) R6: one material per group, flat fills inside. | Each card grows by the inset; one surface per section. |
| C. Inside the card, purple Liquid Glass (literal ask) | A purple glass button inside the card. | HIG Materials: "Don't use Liquid Glass in the content layer" (**should**; the exception is a transient control while activated) and "Use Liquid Glass effects sparingly on custom controls" (**should**). | [surface-materials](surface-materials.md) R2 (glass is navigation-only) and the `apple-glass-consumers` guard; glass over the card's material nests surfaces (R6); no glass before iOS/macOS 26. | Breaks two HIG recommendations and the app's materials rule. |

**Chose B.** No platform **must** applies and the owner asked what is best, so the precedence table prefers the option that keeps the owner's intent (the button belongs to its card) and the web semantics (the CTA is the list's last element) with native, compliant styling: an accent-filled prominent button, not content-layer glass. Contrast (white on `#7C3AED` ≈ 5.7:1), the 44 pt target and label-first names (R3–R5) hold; `CardActionTests`, `GroupedRowsHostedTests` (`actionHeight`, `actionIsFlatPurpleInsideCard`) and `songDetailPreviewEndsCardWithViewFullLeaderboard` pin it. Android and Windows were not part of #382.
