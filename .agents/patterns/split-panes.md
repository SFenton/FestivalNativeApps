# Split panes

> **What:** the boundary between the two panes of a list/detail split: the band between them, whether it draws a line, and the one backdrop running across it. **Read when:** changing a split's divider, band, backdrop or pane margins on any platform, or adding a list/detail split.

Status: **current**, 2026-10-09. Provenance: #344 (split from #332; agent decision); owner rule #361 (where the band sits on iPhone Duo); owner-approved Settings variant #371 (R6); Diagnostics dropped from R6's shared in-list rows by #374 (owner removal; Android done); owner-approved Android on-demand split #583 (R7); owner-approved Windows on-demand split #652 (R7 Windows variant). Layout, geometry and page classification: [split-view.md](../design/apple/split-view.md) (Apple).

## Intent

A split reads as one page that opened an item beside itself, not as two windows. The panes are separated by space (the band and each pane's full-page margins; on a foldable, the fold), never by a drawn splitter, and the page's one backdrop and top darkening run unbroken across the band.

## Web source (behavior reference)

None: the web app has no list/detail split. This is a native layout pattern decided under the design ladder (decision below).

## Rules

1. **R1. No drawn divider.** The band between the panes draws no line, rule or splitter handle. Its width separates the panes: the fold/hinge on a partially folded foldable (Apple `OnDemandSplitPolicy.Geometry.isHinge`; R5), else a 1 pt band at the midpoint plus each pane's full-page margins (20 pt a side on iPad). Owner (#332): "Split View should not have visible vertical splitter component". HIG Layout: "Group related information/functions using negative space, containers, or separators" (should).
2. **R2. Increase Contrast restores a hairline at a midpoint only.** With the system's or the app's Increase Contrast on, a midpoint band draws a 1 pt hairline (Apple `Color.white.opacity(0.16)`); a hinge never does, the fold divides the panes. The band stays decorative: it is never an accessibility element or a splitter. HIG Accessibility: "provide a higher-contrast scheme when Increase Contrast is on" (should). On Apple, `OnDemandSplitLayout` merges the app's toggle (`fst.accessibility.moreContrast`) with `colorSchemeContrast`, as `FestivalGlassSettings` and `ScrollEdgeHardEdge` do. Until #480 it read only the system setting, the same defect #461 fixed in `PinnedFooterBacking`. Tests: hosted `SplitBandAccessibilityTests` (macOS), the iPad journey `IPadShellAccessibilityTests.testSplitBandStaysOutOfTheTreeUnderIncreaseContrastAtAX5` and the iPhone Duo flat and book journeys `DuoSplitBandAccessibilityJourneyTests`, which also prove Close's 44 pt hit region ([apple accessibility](../testing/apple/accessibility.md#shell-structure)).
3. **R3. One backdrop across the band.** The band is clear over the split's single backdrop and carries the panes' top-edge gradient, so image and darkening are continuous from pane to pane (split-view.md "One background, full-page insets").
4. **R4. One component per platform.** Every split draws its band through the canonical layout below; a page never adds its own divider between panes.
5. **R5. The band is on the hinge only in book pose (owner-approved, #361, 2026-10-07).** Owner: "When completely unfolded, midpoint should still be midpoint of free space, not hinge. When partially folded, midpoint should be hinge. This is an override from me, app-wide." In book pose (Apple `pose == .partiallyFolded`, `DeviceLayout.splitHinge`; Android a separating `FoldingFeature`) the panes meet at the fold. Fully unfolded (flat) the split is laid out as on any window of that size, never on a flat hinge line: Apple's on-demand split divides at the midpoint of its free space, the part of its container inside the window's safe area (`DeviceLayout.freeSpan`, the vertical bar excluded; `OnDemandSplitPolicy.freeMidX`), so the two panes' content is equal and the bar stays inside the trailing pane; it is a midpoint band, so R2's Increase Contrast hairline applies. Android's on-demand split (R7) does the same: the midpoint of the content area's safe span when flat, the fold in book posture; Band Detail uses its ordinary list-detail widths, from the free content area. Folding or unfolding re-places the band and reflows the same panes in place, without a reload or a black screen (#346, Apple `OnDemandSplitPolicy.defersWindowChange`). [hinge-columns](hinge-columns.md) R7 is the shared rule. Supersedes split-view.md's flat hinge divider. HIG Designing for iPhone Duo: "Folding region | Present when partially open"; Material 3 foldable postures: "Flat (unfolded): Treat as Medium or Expanded window class based on width"; "Half-opened (book): Split content at the hinge — list on one side, detail on the other". Android evidence (tracker #488): the connected `BandFoldAccessibilityJourneyTest` (CI `android-fold` and `android-device`) publishes flat → half-open → flat folds on Band Detail and Player Bands. It checks that flat panes are equal at the content midpoint and the grid is centred, that half-open splits at the hinge with nothing straddling it, and that both postures keep the reading order, 48 dp targets, 200% text and ATF. Restoring flat-fold anchoring fails it.
6. **R6. Settings list/detail (owner-approved variant, #371, 2026-10-08).** Scope: the Settings page on iPhone Duo unfolded, iPad and Android tablets/foldables (expanded window or book pose); phones and the Mac Settings window (toolbar panes, HIG Settings) keep their layouts, and other splits keep R1–R5 unchanged. Owner: "Settings should align to left and right side should have vertical centered {Settings Icon} under that "Settings" under that "Select a setting to see more options here" … Toggles don't do anything on right side and stay left side only … Show instruments becomes chevron option that opens the toggle list on right … iPhone layout stays the same (Android phone as well)". On those windows Settings is always split: the list on the left at the platform's list-pane width (the fold in book pose, R5), and with nothing selected the right pane shows a vertically centred placeholder (Settings icon, "Settings", "Select a setting to see more options here"). Plain toggles, feedback rows and Reset act in the list and never change the right pane; any other in-list action a platform still has is listed in its paragraph below. Every multi-option setting is a chevron row with a selected state that opens its options in the right pane: Show Instruments, Item Shop, Accessibility, Metadata, Version, Service Info, First Run Guides, Licenses, Privacy Policy, plus the reviewed extras CHOpt Path Default View, CHOpt Text Path Column Order, Song Row Visual Order (while its switch is on) and Maximum Score Leeway (while Filter Invalid Scores is on); a selection whose row disappears returns the pane to the placeholder. Quick Links still scroll the list only. This is an owner exception to the operator's "never an empty 'Select a …' pane" list-detail rule (`.agents/design/android.md`) for Settings only, and it replaces [wide-columns](wide-columns.md) R7's two-column Settings page. HIG Split views: "a split view … to display content in the secondary pane based on a selection in the primary pane"; Material 3 list-detail canonical layout for expanded windows.

   **Apple (iPad and iPhone Duo inner landscape).** Every other list page starts full width and opens its trailing pane on demand; wherever Settings can split, it keeps the trailing pane open. The placeholder's icon is `gearshape`; it uses the shared empty state (`FestivalEmptyState`, [empty-error-states](empty-error-states.md)) and has no Close button. The list keeps the full page's order, and its plain toggles and buttons act in place without changing the right pane: Show Instrument Icons, Enable Independent Song Row Visual Order, Filter Invalid Scores with its leeway slider (one value tied to its toggle), Experimental Ranks, Report an Issue, Request a Feature and Reset (no Diagnostics section, #374).

   Every group with more options is a chevron row, styled like the Licenses link, that opens on the right, highlighted while open. Selecting another row replaces the open one, and Close returns to the placeholder. When an open topic's row leaves the list (Independent Visual Order turned off by its switch or Reset while Song Row Visual Order is open), the list calls `ListDetailSelectAction.close()` and the pane returns to the placeholder (issue #372; `SettingsTopic.isListed(independentVisualOrder:)`, `SettingsScreen.closeUnlistedTopic`). The chevron groups are:

   - Song Row Visual Order (while Independent Visual Order is on)
   - CHOpt Paths (default view and the draggable column order)
   - Accessibility
   - Item Shop
   - Show Instruments
   - Show Instrument Metadata
   - Version
   - Service Info
   - First Run Guides
   - Licenses
   - Privacy Policy (shown inline; a sheet everywhere else)

   A topic page reuses the full page's rows, keys and identifiers. Its card is untitled because the pane's title names the topic, and the description sits above the card. Quick Links stay on the list.

   On Apple this variant does not apply to iPhone, a folded Duo, portrait, compact tiles or the Mac Settings window: they keep the single page, or the Mac's toolbar panes.

   HIG Split views: "Persistently highlight the current selection in each pane leading to a detail view" (should). iOS: "Prefer split views in a regular environment" (should). HIG Lists and tables: "for drill-down, use a disclosure indicator" (should). HIG Settings (macOS): "Choosing Settings from the App menu opens a window, typically a toolbar of related panes" (kept). Implemented by `OnDemandSplitPolicy.ListPage.placeholder` and `trailingContent(cut:allowsSplit:)`, `OnDemandSplitStack.placeholderPane`, `SettingsTopic` and `SettingsScreen(topic:)`. It replaces [wide-columns](wide-columns.md) R7 on iPad and the Duo. Tests: `OnDemandSplitPolicyTests.settingsAcceptsTopicsBesideItsList`, `onlySettingsShowsAPlaceholder`, `trailingContentFollowsSelectionAndPlaceholder`, `SettingsTopicTests`, `selectActionCloseCallsItsHook`, hosted `settingsListClosesSongRowOrderWhenItsSwitchTurnsOff`, `IPadShellAccessibilityTests.testSplitReadingOrderSelectionAndFocus` (`settings`, `settings-licenses`) and `IPadAccessibilityAuditTests` `settings-topic-split`.

   **Platform differences.** Apple groups CHOpt Path Default View and Text Path Column Order into one CHOpt Paths topic and keeps Maximum Score Leeway in the list with its Filter Invalid Scores toggle (one value); Android and Windows open each of those as its own detail. All three show the same placeholder and keep plain toggles in the list. Android has no Diagnostics section in any build (#374), so its list holds only the shared in-list rows.

7. **R7. Android follows the iPhone Duo's on-demand split (owner-approved variant, #583, 2026-10-09).** Owner: "Android in general should follow the layout proposed by iPhone Duo in terms of split views and where and when we use them". Scope: Android tablets, foldables and wide windows; phones, other platforms and Android's own Settings (R6) and Band Detail splits are unchanged. Android uses Apple's page list and window conditions instead of a per-page Material list-detail layout:

   - **Where (the same pages as the Duo's `OnDemandSplitPolicy.ListPage`).** Rivals and All Rivals → Rival Detail; Compete → Rival Detail only (View All and the rankings push full width, #369); Leaderboards → Full Rankings and Band Rankings (#352); Song Detail → the song's instrument board, band board (#367) and Score History. Every other page (Songs, Suggestions, Statistics, Item Shop, profiles, Bands, the boards themselves) stays one full-width page. A list page starts full width and opens its trailing pane only when one of those sub-pages is opened; another item replaces the open one.
   - **When.** Flat: the window is landscape, at least 600 dp wide and 480 dp tall, and each pane keeps at least 360 dp (Apple `minimumPaneWidth`; 360 × font scale from 130% text, the #371 rule). Book posture: a separating vertical fold inside the content area splits there whatever the window's aspect, while each side keeps at least 240 dp (Shop's fold-pane minimum; boards stack their rows below it, #115), because a near-square inner screen held as a book is the Duo's book pose. Portrait tablets, phones, landscape phones and flat near-square foldables stay single-pane, as the Duo does upright or folded. The split stays under TalkBack (Apple keeps it under VoiceOver).
   - **Pane contents.** The open item's own sub-pages (Rivalry, another board or page of a board) push inside the pane with Back; everything else (profiles, songs, tabs, search results) opens full width over the split, and Back returns to it (#352). The pane's first page shows Close instead of Back; global search, the bell and the avatar stay in the list page's top app bar only. No row stays highlighted (Android list rows have no selected style). A song board at the pane's root drops the song header ([song-leaderboard-header](song-leaderboard-header.md) Variants, #342).
   - **Back ([back-keeps-place](back-keeps-place.md) R6).** System Back and the list page's Back pop the pane's own stack, then close the pane, then leave the page; an open drawer or global search still closes first.
   - **Window changes.** When the window stops allowing a split (rotating to portrait, folding, 130%+ text), the pane's pages become ordinary pushed pages above the list page, with their routes and saved state; when it starts allowing one, the sub-pages pushed above a list page move into its pane (Apple `defersWindowChange`). The leading pane's width animates over 300 ms (instant under Reduce Motion).
   - **Boundary.** R1–R5 apply: no divider (a hairline at a midpoint only under Increase Contrast), the fold in book posture, the content's safe-span midpoint when flat, the one backdrop across the band.
   - **Guidance.** Material 3 list-detail: "Compact: List view OR detail view / Medium: Side-by-side list (1/3) + detail (2/3)" (should; the owner's Duo policy chooses equal panes and the Duo's pages instead); foldable postures: "Half-opened (book): Split content at the hinge — list on one side, detail on the other" (kept); "Never place interactive content or critical information across the hinge area" (must; kept: each pane ends at the fold).
   - **Page tools.** The trailing pane hosts the shared `FloatingToolbar` with its own `FloatingToolbarHost` (same `fst.nav.floating-toolbar` tag) at the pane's bottom end, for the page inside it; the list page's tools stay in the shell's toolbar over the list pane ([page-tools-and-nav-chrome](page-tools-and-nav-chrome.md) R4). Neither toolbar hides or minimizes while either pane scrolls ([page-tools-and-nav-chrome](page-tools-and-nav-chrome.md) R7, #577).
   - **Tests.** `OnDemandSplitPolicyTest`; Robolectric `OnDemandSplitLandscapeUiTest`, `OnDemandSplitPortraitUiTest`, `OnDemandSplitBookFoldUiTest`, `RivalsSplitUiTest`; connected `SplitPaneAccessibilityJourneyTest` (`@HalfOpenFoldJourney`: half open, flat, 200% text, reading order, Close target, ATF).

   **Windows variant (owner-approved, #652, 2026-10-13).** Owner: "Split view should follow the same rules as iPhone Duo/iPad (e.g. songs shouldn't click and then we see songs on right side- align with iOS here); As screen gets wider, the size of each "half" doesn't change/get bigger or smaller". Scope: the Windows app; it replaces the page-local fixed-width splits (#371-era Songs, All Rivals, Full Rankings and Band Rankings at a 1100 epx page with a 520/560 epx list column and an auto-selected first row). Windows follows R7's page list and pane contents with these Windows conditions:

   - **Where.** The same list pages and sub-pages as R7 (`OnDemandSplitPolicy.Accepts`): Rivals and All Rivals → Rival Detail (Compete opens the Rivals root on Windows, which has no Compete section); Leaderboards → Full Rankings and Band Rankings; Song Detail → the instrument board, the band board and Score History. Songs pushes Song Detail full width, like every other page; no row is pre-selected or stays highlighted (R7). Settings keeps R6 with the same equal halves.
   - **When.** Equal halves of the section's content area (the page area right of the navigation pane) whenever each half keeps at least 480 epx (`OnDemandSplitPolicy.MinimumPaneWidth`, × the Windows text scale from 130%, the #371 rule), so a 1280 epx window with the expanded pane splits and `medium` (900 epx) does not. The panes are star columns, so both grow and shrink with the window (Fluent responsive design: "reposition, resize, reflow"). Windows has no orientation or fold, so width and text scale are the only conditions.
   - **Window changes (agent decision #652).** Narrowing below the split with a pane open keeps the pane's page full width and hides the list (Fluent list/details "stacked" mode: "only one pane is visible at a time"); the title-bar Back then closes the pane. Widening shows both halves again. Pages pushed full width while narrow stay pushed when the window widens (no re-expansion: the Frame's back stack is not rebuilt), unlike Android's `defersWindowChange` move.
   - **Pane chrome.** The pane's top bar holds a Close button (Segoe Fluent `E711`, 40×40 epx, Name "Close", `fst.split.close`); the pane is a UIA group named "Detail" (`fst.split.pane`) after the list in reading order. The pane's own sub-pages (R7's) push inside it; profiles, songs, bands and other pages open full width over the split, and Back returns to the split. A launch deep link (`--route`) opens its page full width. A song board, band board or Score History at the pane's root beside Song Detail drops the song header and is titled by its board ([song-leaderboard-header](song-leaderboard-header.md) Variants, #667; `OnDemandSplitHost.ApplyPaneTitle`, re-applied on every pane navigation and layout pass, so the stacked pane shows the song header again).
   - **Back ([back-keeps-place](back-keeps-place.md) R6).** Title-bar Back, Alt+Left, the mouse Back button and the Back key pop the pane's stack, then close the pane, then pop the section; closing returns focus to the row or button that opened the pane when focus was inside it. Re-invoking the section closes every pane.
   - **Boundary.** R1–R3: no divider, a 1 epx `FSTRowSeparatorBrush` hairline at the midpoint only under Increase Contrast (the app's toggle or a Windows contrast theme; Raw accessibility view, not hit-testable), the shell's one backdrop across both halves (the cover of whichever pane shows a song page).
   - **Motion (#669, [reflow-motion](reflow-motion.md)).** Opening and closing animate on the compositor over 300 ms, as Android's leading-width animation: the list changes width once, the pane slides in from its trailing edge while fading in, and on close slides out and fades over the widened list, which an animated clip uncovers. Instant under Reduce Motion or Windows Animation effects off. Stacking and unstacking on a window resize stay instant.
   - **Guidance.** Fluent list/details: "The list/details pattern has a list pane (usually with a list view) and a details pane for content. When an item in the list is selected, the details pane is updated" and side-by-side versus stacked by width (should; kept with the owner's page set and equal panes).
   - **Tests.** `OnDemandSplitPolicyTests`, `OnDemandSplitMarkupTests`, `SettingsDetailsTests`; journeys `tools/windows/journeys/split-panes.json` (Songs pushes; Rivals, Leaderboards → Band Rankings and Song Detail open beside the list; Close and Back close the pane; narrowing stacks, widening restores; `medium` pushes) and `a11y-split-panes.json` (Close name and 40 epx target, list → pane reading order, Axe scan, 225% text pushes full width).

Agent decision (#344, 2026-10-07; owner may override with `/choose`): options were (A) no line on the Duo only, keeping the 1 pt hairline on iPad and Mac per HIG Split views macOS "Prefer the 1 pt thin divider" (prefer); (B) no line on any split; (C) B plus the hairline under Increase Contrast at a midpoint. Chose **C**: the owner's words name no platform and an owner choice outranks a platform *prefer*; the macOS clause picks a style for *draggable* dividers, and this split is fixed at the midpoint; Increase Contrast keeps an accessible boundary for people who ask for one. Supersedes: split-view.md "Prefer the 1 pt thin divider (kept)".

**R6 on Windows (owner-approved variant, #371, 2026-10-08; halves #652).** Windows has no Duo or tablet class, so R6's wide-window scope is a Settings page wide enough for two halves of at least 480 epx each (`SettingsDetails.UsesSplit`, delegating to `OnDemandSplitPolicy.AllowsSplit` with the Windows text scale, the R7 Windows variant's rule); narrower pages keep the one-column page unchanged. The list and the detail pane are equal star halves that resize with the window (#652; until then a fixed 520 epx list column from a 1100 epx page), with no drawn divider (R1) over the shell's one backdrop (R3). The placeholder reuses the Rivals empty-state composition: the Settings `FontIcon` (U+E713, 40 epx), a Subtitle heading and the secondary message. Chevron rows are `SettingsDetailRow`: the feedback rows' look, a value beside the chevron for CHOpt Path Default View and Leeway, and on the open row the leaderboard current-row fill (`FSTCurrentRowBrush`) with UIA ItemStatus "Selected". Opening a section moves its one live card into the pane, so bindings, drag lists and Service Info polling keep working. Licenses opens `LicensesPage` in the pane's frame, and Privacy Policy shows its text inline instead of the dialog. Narrowing to one column moves the card back and keeps the selection for the next widening. Focus survives the switch (#571): a focused control that stays shown keeps focus (a moved card back in the list), a focused chevron row hands focus to its section's first control in one column, and a focused section control to its chevron row in the split, with `FocusState.Programmatic` (no keyboard tooltip) and brought into view once the new layout settles; open tooltips inside the list close first (`CollapsedToolTip`, [windows.md](../platforms/windows.md#gotchas); journeys `settings-split-swap-wide`/`-narrow` in `a11y-settings-swap.json`, which skip 225% text, where a wide window has no split; `settings-split-large-text` checks that the page stays one column there and the focused switch keeps focus across the resize, #658). Quick Links stay the header menu and scroll the list: in the split each section's Quick Links ID moves to its chevron row, so a link lands on the row and focuses it without opening it. The side Quick Links pane never shows on Settings, because its 1150 epx threshold is above the split's 960 epx. Canonical: `windows/Festival.Core/Domain/SettingsDetails.cs` `SettingsDetails` (entries, split width, availability, Quick Links IDs); `windows/Festival.App/Pages/SettingsPage.xaml.cs` (`ApplyDetail`, `ApplyQuickLinkAnchors`); `windows/Festival.App/Controls/SettingsDetailRow.xaml.cs` `SettingsDetailRow`. Tests: `SettingsDetailsTests`, `SettingsDetailMarkupTests`; hosted journeys `settings-split-details`, `settings-split-row-gone` and `settings-split-narrow` (`tools/windows/journeys/settings-split.json`: each owner-named row and the four App Settings rows open their title and content, the open row reads "Selected", plain switches keep the placeholder and the open pane, Licenses and inline Privacy Policy, a hidden row returns the placeholder, narrowing restores the one-column page and widening the selection), and the Quick Links journeys `ql-split-jumped` and `ql-split-order` (`quick-links.json`), `qll-settings-split` and `qlo-split-settings` (all wide and maximized at display 150%). Windows list/details pattern: "The list/details pattern has a list pane (usually with a list view) and a details pane for content. When an item in the list is selected, the details pane is updated."
