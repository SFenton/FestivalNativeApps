# Song header

> **What:** the song identity block at the top of song pages (album art beside a one-line marquee title, artist · year · length, optional third line) and its compact pinned form in the navigation bar. **Read when:** adding or changing a song page header, a song title or artist at the top of a page or in its bar, or the shared marquee text.

Status: **current**, 2026-10-05. Provenance: #315.

## Intent

A song page names its song once, the same way everywhere, at full width, like the web's `SongInfoHeader`. The title and artist each sit on one line that fills the width beside the album art, so a title never wraps early into a narrow column or stops early with empty space to its right. A line that does not fit scrolls (marquee) inside its column; a line that fits stays still. When the header scrolls under the bar, a compact copy (small art and the title) takes the bar's title slot.

## Rules

1. **R1. One shared header.** Every song page (Song Details, Song Leaderboard, Band Song Leaderboard, Player History) draws its song identity, its leaderboard header row and its bar title through the canonical components below (title, artist · year · length, optional third line such as "Duos · N entries"), never a page-local title `Text`, link, `TextBlock` or copied header row. A tappable title opens the song (web `onTitleClick`), and the whole header is one control with the heading role.
2. **R2. Fill the width, one line, marquee only on overflow.** The header's text column takes all the width beside the art; the title and artist never wrap at standard text sizes and never tail-truncate while motion is allowed. A line wider than its column scrolls with the platform's shared marquee (Apple `MarqueeText`, Android `FestivalMarqueeText`, Windows `MarqueeText`); lines that fit stay still. The header's overflowing lines scroll in lockstep (web `useMarqueeSync`). (#315: the Apple Song Leaderboard wrapped "Through the Fire / and Flames" beside empty space.)
3. **R3. Motion and large text follow the shared marquee.** With Reduce Motion / Remove animations the line tail-truncates without animation (HIG Accessibility: "When Reduce Motion is on, reduce automatic and repetitive animation"). At accessibility text sizes the in-page header wraps onto as many lines as it needs so the whole name is readable (HIG Typography, *should*: "Minimize text truncation at large font sizes in scrollable regions"). Assistive technology reads the full title once.
4. **R4. One pinned bar title, one line.** When the header scrolls away, the compact bar copy (small art where the platform shows it, the title on one marqueeing line, optional caption such as the instrument or band size) takes the bar's title slot. It stays on one line at every text size (Apple `marqueeWrapsAtAccessibilitySizes = false`), uses the bar's available width rather than a fixed cap, is built only while the in-page header is scrolled away (so it is never read twice) and comes from the canonical component, like the web's collapsed `SongInfoHeader`. Bar actions that no longer fit still move to the overflow menu. (#315 review: a 240 pt cap left the bar's slot half empty and started the marquee early.)

| Pinned bar title | `apple/Sources/FestivalUI/Common/SongHeaderText.swift` `SongBarTitle`, through `SongBarTitleToolbarItem` on Song Details and both boards (principal item, empty unspoken placeholder until the header scrolls away; iOS/iPadOS) | `android/app/src/main/java/com/festivalscoretracker/android/ui/common/FestivalScreen.kt` `marqueeTitle` | `FSTMarqueeTitleStyle` on `SongDetailPage` |

**Apple R4 implementation:** `SongBarTitle` sets no width frame. SwiftUI measures a `.principal` toolbar item with an unbounded proposal, so `.frame(maxWidth: .infinity)` collapses it to a point and a fixed `maxWidth` caps it; with no frame the bar fits the title's natural width between its leading and trailing items, and a short title stays centred. The boards decide when the header has scrolled away from their scroll view's offset (`View.songHeaderScrollAway(headerBottom:)`, `SongHeaderScrollAway.isHidden`: offset ≥ space above the header + its measured height), never from the header row's own geometry: a `List` recycles the header's row as it leaves the screen, so the Song Leaderboard's row stopped reporting before it passed the bar and the title never pinned. Song Details uses the same reader with its hero title's bottom edge, measured in the scroll content (`songHeaderContentSpace()`), not in `.scrollView` space, which moves when the bar lays out again. **macOS (agent decision, #315; owner may override with `/choose`):** the pinned bar title is iOS/iPadOS only, as Song Details and Full Rankings already were. An `NSToolbar` gives a centred SwiftUI item its ideal width (a flexible `maxWidth` does not grow it), so an uncapped long title pushed the trailing Quick Links action into overflow in a 1,100 pt window, while a fixed cap is what R4 forbids. HIG Toolbars: trailing items are "important always-available items" that "remain visible at every window size" (should). The Mac window title already names the song at the toolbar's leading edge (`festivalNavigationTitle(song.title)` on all three pages), so they skip the centred copy there.

**Apple consumers:** Song Details hero and pinned title (`SongDetailScreen`, its own 96 pt top-aligned row around `SongHeaderText`); Song Leaderboard (`SoloLeaderboardScreen`, caption = instrument) and Band Song Leaderboard (`SongBandLeaderboardScreen`, caption = band size) through `SongHeaderRow` + `SongBarTitleToolbarItem`, with the board line from `SongLeaderboardBoardLine` (web `songBandLeaderboard.subtitle`; [song-leaderboard-header](song-leaderboard-header.md) R1, R2). The #317 board header (`SongLeaderboardHeader`, `SongLeaderboardPinnedTitle`, a wrapping title and a 240 pt bar cap) was folded into these components when #315 merged, so both boards keep #316/#317's paging, reload gate and anchors with one header; Player History (`PlayerHistoryScreen`, caption "<Instrument> · Score History" with the instrument icon) through `SongHeaderRow` + `SongBarTitleToolbarItem` (#324); in a split it is Song Details' trailing pane. All on iPhone, iPad, iPhone Duo and Mac.

**Windows consumers** of `SongLeaderboardHeader` (#317): Song Leaderboard (`Pages/LeaderboardsSongPage`, board line = instrument icon + name) and Band Song Leaderboard (`Pages/BandsSongLeaderboardPage`, board line = band size), both opening Song Detail from the header like the web's `onTitleClick`.

**Android consumers** of `SongHeader`: Song Detail, Song Leaderboard and Song Band Leaderboard (`artSize = 64.dp`; the band board adds `onTitleClick` → Song Detail and, since #317, names its band size through the solo board's `SongBoardSwitcher` instead of a "Duos · N entries" third line: [song-leaderboard-header](song-leaderboard-header.md)). Player History keeps its compact instrument-icon row and puts the title in `FestivalMarqueeText` (no art; see Known debt). `FestivalScreen(marqueeTitle = true)` is used by Song Detail, Song Leaderboard and Song Band Leaderboard; other pages keep the Material 3 truncating title.

**Agent decision (#315, 2026-10-05): the song cover is the backdrop of every page with a song header; owner may override with `/choose`.** The Apple Band Song Leaderboard was the one song page on the shell's rotating carousel backdrop; giving it the song header raised whether it keeps the carousel. The owner's "Full width and carousel" means the title's marquee, not the backdrop.

| Option | What you'd see | Guidance (strength) | Web / pattern precedent | Trade-offs |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A. Keep the carousel | The shell's rotating album-art carousel behind the Band Song Leaderboard, under its song header | HIG Materials: "thinner, more translucent materials preserve context by showing more background" (consider); nothing requires either backdrop | Apple only (undocumented); no other platform or the web | Smallest diff for a title bug, but the page's backdrop moves on its own behind a marqueeing title and shows other songs' covers under one song's header |
| B. Song cover (chosen) | The song's own dimmed cover, as on Song Details and the Song Leaderboard | HIG Materials: "Use Liquid Glass for controls and navigation… Let content scroll and peek through while preserving control and navigation legibility" (should; holds over either backdrop); HIG Toolbars: "Reduce backgrounds/tints that interfere with system effects; let content set appearance" (consider) | Web `pages/leaderboard/band/SongBandLeaderboardPage.tsx` `background={<PageBackground src={song?.albumArt} />}`; Android `SongBandLeaderboardScreen` `background.pushFocus(song?.albumArt)`; Windows `BandsSongLeaderboardPage` `BackdropArt => Song.AlbumArt`; Apple `SoloLeaderboardScreen` `.festivalBackground(.song(albumArt))` | A visible change beyond the title wrap; the backdrop stays still while the title scrolls, and the page matches its siblings |

Chose B: web behavior beats an undocumented native copy (consistency-sweep precedence), and Android, Windows and the Apple Song Leaderboard already show the song's cover. Implemented as `festivalBackground(.song(song.albumArt))` in `SongBandLeaderboardScreen`; #317 reached the same choice independently and made it a rule ([song-leaderboard-header](song-leaderboard-header.md) R4).

**Agent decision (#315, 2026-10-05; the owner may override):** on Android the song pages' top-app-bar title scrolls instead of ending in "…". Options: (A) keep Material 3's default one-line truncation; (B) wrap in a taller bar; (C) one-line marquee. Chose C. Web (collapsed `SongInfoHeader`), Apple (pinned `MarqueeText`) and Windows (`FSTMarqueeTitleStyle`) already scroll this title. Material 3 keeps a small top app bar to one Title-style line ("Top app bar title | Title Large"; small bar "Start-aligned, 64dp"), which C keeps and B breaks. Remove animations still truncates (R3).

## Guards (`tools/pattern_guard.py`)

- `song-header/apple-wrapping-title`: a page-local `Text(song.title)` in an Apple feature view. Song list rows legitimately use plain one-line text (`SongRowView` is allowed), so the guard can't cover every platform; review other pages against R1–R4.

Regression journeys: Apple `SongHeaderTextTests` lay out the shared column and leaderboard row and assert R2 (a long title stays on one line and the column fills the width beside the art), R3 (in-page wrap at accessibility sizes; one heading that names the song once) and R4 (`SongBarTitle` fills a 258 pt phone slot and a 640 pt wide slot, past the former 240 pt cap, while a short title hugs; it stays one line at `.accessibility5`; `songHeaderScrollAway` flips in a `List` and a `ScrollView` on both scroll-reading paths, and Song Details' hero threshold stays fixed while it scrolls). The iOS `SongHeaderJourneyTests` (fixture `mock_service.py --long-titles --port 18950`; landscape on iPad) scroll the Song Leaderboard and the Band Song Leaderboard until the title pins, then assert it reads the full title once and spans the bar to within 32 pt of its nearest leading and trailing items with every action still hittable. Song Details shows the same `SongBarTitleToolbarItem` but has no journey: after a swipe XCUITest cannot query its pinned title even though it draws (also failing on master's `SongDetailJourneyTests.testSongDetailPinnedTitleAndCardLayout`, 2026-10-06), so the hosted tests carry its width. Every Android `FestivalMarqueeText` publishes what it draws as the semantics property `FestivalMarquee.ModeKey` (`Static`, `Scrolling`, `Truncated`, `Wrapped`; not read by TalkBack). Android `ui/songdetail/SongHeaderTitleUiTest` launches the whole shell on Song Detail, Song Leaderboard, Song Band Leaderboard and Player History with a synthetic overflowing title and asserts R2 (one `Scrolling` line whose box ends at the header's edge), R3 (Reduce Motion `Truncated` with an ellipsis; 200% text `Wrapped` in-page; one heading stop that reads the title once), R4 (after scrolling, the bar title is one `Scrolling` line up to the first action, also at 200%) and that short titles stay `Static`. A plain `Text` title has no mode and fails them. Add a page here when it gains a song header. #317 added the band board's pinned bar title to `songBandLeaderboardTitleScrollsAcrossTheHeaderAndTheBar`.
