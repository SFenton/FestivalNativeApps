# Global search — iPhone notes

> **What:** the Apple design and implementation of global search (songs, players, bands) on iPhone: a trailing **Search tab** in the tab bar (issue #92). **Read when:** changing the search entry point, surface or results on iPhone. Behavior and test IDs: [spec.md](spec.md). iPad: [ipados.md](ipados.md); Mac: [macos.md](macos.md). Placement of every page control: [nav-accessories.md](../../design/apple/nav-accessories.md).

## Decision per layout

| Layout | Entry point | Surface |
|---|---|---|
| iPhone (iOS 18+) | Trailing `Tab(value: RootTab.search, role: .search)`: the separate round Search button beside the tab capsule on iOS 26 | `GlobalSearchTab`: system `.searchable` field focused on selection (keyboard up), scope bar and results below |
| iPhone Duo folded (vertical bar) | The Search tab, separate at the end of the rail | `GlobalSearchTab`: system `.searchable` field across the bottom |
| iPhone Duo inner display (flat or book-folded) | The Search tab in the rail (landscape) or tab bar (portrait) | `GlobalSearchTab` with `BottomSearchField` (#349): the field at the bottom, focused when the tab appears; it and the scope bar share one column, full width flat and the right page in book pose. Landscape results show two cards per row meeting at the hinge, flat or folded; portrait one column (#350, [wide-columns](../../patterns/wide-columns.md) R1, R3) |
| iPhone iOS 17 | Search tab via `.tag(RootTab.search)` (no search role) | `GlobalSearchTab` |
| Any, hardware keyboard | ⌘K or ⌘F select the Search tab | `GlobalSearchTab` |

Issue #92 (owner, 2026-10-04) replaced the header Search button and the tab-bar accessory dock with the Search tab. HIG Search fields (`apple-hig/references/hig/search-fields.md`): a separate trailing tab "focuses the field and opens the keyboard immediately… choose it for fast, transient search that returns to the previous tab on exit"; HIG Tab bars (`tab-bars.md`): "A dedicated Search tab may be trailing." There is **no other** global-search entry point on iPhone (no header button, accessory item or floating capsule); the floating page tools hide while the Search tab is open (#632).

**Transitions** (`RootTabTransition`, `App/Shell/RootTab.swift`, unit-tested): the section underneath stays selected while Search shows. The system **Close** beside the field (or tapping that section) returns to it with its stack intact; a result is pushed on it; choosing a different section leaves Search for that section. iOS 26 also focuses the field itself (`tabViewSearchActivation(.searchTabSelection)`). The model (query, scope, results) lives in the shell, but the system clears the text on Close.

**Title (issue #100):** the page keeps its large **Search** navigation title while the field is active, like every other root tab's title. Choosing the tab activates the field at once, and `.searchable` hides the navigation bar on activation by default, so the title never showed; `GlobalSearchTab` applies `.searchPresentationToolbarBehavior(.avoidHidingContent)` (iOS 17.1+), as `ProfileSelectionSheet` does. HIG VoiceOver (`voiceover.md`): "Give each page or screen a unique, succinct title describing its content and purpose; assistive technology announces it first." HIG Searching (`searching.md`): "Show current scope with descriptive placeholder, scope bar, or title". The title stays large while results scroll (the results `List` sits under the scope bar, not at the stack root). `GlobalSearchJourneyTests.testSearchTabFocusesFieldAndCloseReturnsToSongs` asserts the hittable title with the keyboard up and after typing. **Accessibility test (#450):** `GlobalSearchAccessibilityJourneyTests` runs in CI against the large-catalogue fixture on `:8765` (`FST_SEARCH_A11Y_FIXTURE_URL` overrides it): on iPhone, Duo folded (`…DuoFolded`, the `DebugDuoWindow` simulation) and the iPad sidebar page at AX5, with the field active and with results, the title must stay a hittable static text "Search" in the bar, in the top third, above and read before the scope bar and results, recognized whole and grown from its default line height: more than 1.35× on the iPad page, 1.15× for the phone tab's in-bar `inlineLarge` title (#611; UIKit caps bar-hosted titles, measured 1.21× on iOS 27, `system-bar-title-size`). The field must be hittable, and on phone Close must be at least 44×44 pt. On the real iPhone Duo device type (folded, iOS 27.1; locally `uitest --device duo`), UIKit caps the bar's large title at 1.10× (`system-bar-title-size`), so the floor there is 1.05×. That device's vertical bar shows no Close, so Close is not checked. `performAccessibilityAudit` must pass for Dynamic Type, clipped text, descriptions and contrast. The only accepted issues are the system field's single-line placeholder, `system-search-placeholder-clipped` and `-contrast` in [accessibility.md](../../testing/apple/accessibility.md); iPad at AX5 clips it. Removing `KeepSearchTitleWhileSearching` fails it. Keep the journey on the CI lists whenever the Search title or chrome changes. HIG Typography (`typography.md`): "Keep text truncation to a minimum as font size increases"; Accessibility (`accessibility.md`): 44×44 pt.

**Title position (issue #611):** the Search tab has no bar items, so the standard large-title layout left an empty bar row above **Search** (the reported dead space). The phone and Duo Search tab use the system `toolbarTitleDisplayMode(.inlineLarge)` (`GlobalSearchTitleStyle.resolve(asTab:)`): the same large title sits in the bar row with the scope bar directly beneath it. The iPad sidebar Search page keeps the standard mode because its bar row holds the bell and Profile. Agent decision recorded in [page-tools-and-nav-chrome](../../patterns/page-tools-and-nav-chrome.md) R14. `GlobalSearchJourneyTests` asserts no empty band (title top within 12 pt of the bar, scope bar within 24 pt below) in every state; `testSearchTitleSitsInBarRowAtLargestText` checks it at AX5 with an accessibility audit.

**Five-tab limit:** the search tab counts toward the iPhone's five tabs (iOS 26.5: five sections + Search showed **More**). `FestivalTabPolicy.fittingSearchTab` drops Statistics from compact phone tabs (player: Songs · Suggestions · Compete · Settings · Search); the drawer's Statistics row pushes it. The iPhone Duo inner display uses the same fitted set as folded (#337).

`GlobalSearchSheet` (own `GlobalSearchField`, inline title, Close) is now used only by the Mac window. `GlobalSearchField` itself (with `surface: .floating`) is also the iPhone Duo bottom search field, `BottomSearchField`: Songs' Filter field (#333) and the Search tab's field on the inner display (#349), so the app has one custom search-field look. On iOS its whole capsule is the field's touch target and Clear Search is a 44 × 44 pt button (#474; tested in every Duo pose by #475; [pattern R2 and R25](../../patterns/page-tools-and-nav-chrome.md)).

**iPhone Duo inner display (#349; [pattern R2](../../patterns/page-tools-and-nav-chrome.md)):** beside the vertical bar the system field took only the bottom trailing ~370 pt and rose centred over the keyboard, out of line with the full-width scope bar, and no system search API sets its width. So `GlobalSearchFieldPlacement.resolve(pose:asTab:)` gives the Search tab `BottomSearchField` there (identifier `fst.global-search.field`, clear `fst.global-search.clear`, the scope's prompt, Return re-runs the search). The scope bar takes the same column (`bottomSearchFieldColumn(hinge:)`): owner, full bottom width when flat and the right page when book-folded; agent decision, the scope bar shares that column at the top of the results. Rows fade above the field (`BottomSearchFieldFade`). With no system field there is no system Close: the rail or tab bar leaves Search. HIG: Search fields (`search-fields.md`), "Place search at the bottom if there's room" and "Consider a scope bar in the results area"; Designing for iPhone Duo (`designing-for-iphone-duo.md`), "use reserved-region APIs to keep important elements clear of the center" and "Keep controls near the content they affect". Tests: `BottomSearchFieldPlacementTests` (policy, fold rule) and hosted `searchScopeBarAndBottomFieldShareOneColumn` (flat and book).

## Songs: filter stays separate

Songs keeps its own list filter: the **inline** `.searchable(placement: .navigationBarDrawer(displayMode: .always))` field with the prompt "Filter Songs", pinned above the list. It filters the list together with sort, filters and the A–Z scrubber. HIG Search fields: "Use an inline field when adjacency clarifies that it filters one view rather than searches globally"; Music does the same (global Search tab plus an inline Library filter). Global search never reads or writes the Songs text, and vice versa ([spec](spec.md#page-local-search-songs-is-separate)).

## Implementation

| Piece | File |
|---|---|
| Pure rules: scopes, prompts, ≥2 chars, 250 ms debounce, ≤20 songs in catalogue order, ≤10 players | `FestivalCore/GlobalSearch.swift` (+ `GlobalSearchTests`) |
| `GlobalSearchModel`: query, scope, per-section state, cancellation via `.task(id: runKey)`, catalogue reloaded per publication | `Features/Search/GlobalSearchModel.swift` |
| `GlobalSearchResults`, `GlobalSearchSheet` (Mac), `GlobalSearchButton` (Mac), `PageTrailingItems` | `Features/Search/GlobalSearchView.swift` |
| `GlobalSearchEmptyStateView` (wraps the shared `FestivalEmptyState`, #377) | `Features/Search/GlobalSearchEmptyState.swift` |
| `FestivalEmptyState`, `FoldAvoidingPlacement` (+ `FoldAvoidingPlacementTests`, `FestivalEmptyStateTests`) | `Common/FestivalEmptyState.swift` |
| `GlobalSearchTab`, `RootTabBarBehavior` | `Features/Search/GlobalSearchTab.swift` |
| `RootTab`, `RootTabTransition` (+ `RootTabTransitionTests`) | `App/Shell/RootTab.swift` |
| Search tab, ⌘K/⌘F, `openGlobalSearch()`, iPad sidebar search detail | `App/FestivalRootView.swift` |

- **Scope bar:** a segmented `Picker` (All · Songs · Players · Bands), the HIG scope-bar form with the broadest scope first, instead of web toggle chips. The container carries `fst.global-search.scope`; segments are reached by label (a system segmented control has no per-segment identifier).
- **Short query (issue #299):** under two characters a centred `GlobalSearchEmptyStateView` hint names the scope (`GlobalSearch.enterQueryHint(for:)`): "Enter at least two characters to search for songs, players, or bands." (All), "… for songs.", "… for players.", "… for bands.".
- **Loading (issue #299):** one `FestivalLoadingView("Searching")` (`fst.global-search.loading`) fills the results area, so it is centred horizontally and vertically between the scope bar and the field/keyboard/tab bar, as the app's other pages centre theirs. `GlobalSearch.isSearching(scope:outcomes:)` decides: All waits for Songs, Players and Bands; a single scope waits only for itself (Songs never waits for the network). No inline Players progress (`players-loading` is gone). HIG Progress indicators (`progress-indicators.md`): "Display progress indicators in a consistent location".
  - Filling placement follows [load-transition](../../patterns/load-transition.md) R13: the same result-region centre as before on iPhone, iPad and Mac; the existing empty-state fold policy on a partially folded Duo (SFenton/festival-report-tracker#624). HIG `references/hig/designing-for-iphone-duo.md`: "use reserved-region APIs to keep important elements clear of the center" (should).
- **Errors (issue #299):** per section via `ServiceStatusInline(showsRetryButton: false, fallbackTitle:)` (`fst.global-search.{songs,players,bands}-error`): a generic failure reads "Players unavailable" or "Bands unavailable" (`GlobalSearch.unavailableTitle(for:)`) with its message, and a scrape freeze still reads "Scores are updating" and re-runs itself when its countdown ends. No Retry button: submitting the same text (keyboard Search, Return on the Mac sheet) re-runs a shown failed section or an empty player or band result (`GlobalSearch.submitReruns`, `GlobalSearchModel.submit()`), and editing the query re-runs it. HIG Progress indicators: "If a process stalls, explain the problem and what people can do".
- **Empty state (issue #99):** `GlobalSearchEmptyStateView` (`Features/Search/GlobalSearchEmptyState.swift`), with copy from `GlobalSearch.emptyState(scope:query:)`. A decorative magnifier, a `.title2` bold title ("No Players Found", "No Songs Found", "No Bands Found", "No Results Found"), a `.body` subtitle (the [spec](spec.md#states-and-motion) copy), and no button (issue #299 removed **Retry**; see Errors). It is centred horizontally and vertically in the area below the scope bar. In All an empty Songs, Players or Bands section is hidden while another has results (web parity), so the old left-aligned "No players found. / Retry" row is gone. It is one static-text VoiceOver element, "title. subtitle", with the id `fst.global-search.hint`. Not `ContentUnavailableView`: like `ServiceStatusView`, it uses GeometryReader + ScrollView + `minHeight`, so accessibility sizes wrap and scroll instead of truncating. iPhone Duo: `FoldAvoidingPlacement` centres it in the larger side of a fold that crosses the results area. HIG: Writing (`writing.md`): "Provide clear next steps on any blank screens, with a button or link if possible" (the subtitle gives the next step; the field is the control); Typography (`typography.md`): "Make sure your layout adapts to all font sizes"; VoiceOver (`voiceover.md`): "Specify how elements are grouped"; iPhone Duo (`designing-for-iphone-duo.md`): keep important elements clear of the centre (fold). Live checks: iPhone 17 Pro (default and AX XXXL), iPad Pro 11, iPhone Duo folded; macOS shares the view (`mac_app.py build`).
- **Bands (issue #320):** `GlobalSearchModel` calls `FestivalAPI.searchBands` (`BandSearch.swift`, `GET /api/bands/search?q=&page=1&pageSize=10`, validated whole) together with the player search after the debounce, in every scope (web `useUnifiedSearch`). Results reuse the player-bands card, `PlayerBandRow(open:, identifier: "fst.global-search.result.band")` (web `PlayerBandCard`: member names, instrument icons, "N songs together"); the `open` action closes Search before the band page (`AppRoute.band`) pushes, as song and player results do. "All" lists bands after players and its prompt is "Search songs, players, or bands" (HIG Search fields, `search-fields.md`: "Use placeholder text to state what is searchable"). Natives have no selected band, so a band never opens Statistics. The earlier explanation card and Band Rankings button are gone.
- **Result-count announcement:** once the sections the scope shows have settled, VoiceOver hears `AccessibilityNotification.Announcement` with "3 songs, 1 player, 2 bands", "No results found.", "No bands found." or "1 song, Players unavailable, 1 band" (`GlobalSearch.resultAnnouncement`, `GlobalSearchModel.resultAnnouncement`). It is debounced (`GlobalSearchResults.announcementDelay`, 700 ms in a `.task(id:)` keyed by the text), so typing or a second section arriving speaks only the last count; queries under two characters announce nothing.
- **Result cards (batch 6, Lane A3):** a plain `List` of separate glass cards 4pt apart on 16pt margins (inset-grouped doubled the left inset); song cards reuse the Songs row's 44pt art, `.headline` title and "artist · year · duration" subtitle so both rows are the same height; All lists song cards, then player cards, then band cards. **Section titles (issue #348, [section-headers](../../patterns/section-headers.md) R9):** in All, each section with rows or a failure starts with a `FestivalSectionHeader` row ("Songs", "Players", "Bands"; `.isHeader`, `fst.global-search.section.{songs,players,bands}`), inset 4pt inside the cards' margin with 16pt above every title but the first. It is a List row, not a pinned `Section` header, because the web `<h3>` scrolls with its rows. `GlobalSearch.showsSectionTitle(_:scope:outcomes:)` decides: empty and pending sections and their titles are omitted (web `shouldRenderGlobalSection`), and a single scope has none, because the scope bar names it (issue #299; HIG Searching, `searching.md`: "Show current scope with descriptive placeholder, scope bar, or title"). Each title takes one fade slot before its rows (`GlobalSearch.staggerStart(of:scope:outcomes:)`, web `getTargetStaggerSlotCount`). HIG Search fields (`search-fields.md`): "Put the most relevant results first; categorization can reduce scrolling" (consider). VoiceOver's heading rotor moves between Songs, Players and Bands. On the Mac the sheet's Close is `FestivalSheetCloseItem`. The result-count announcement is VoiceOver-only; no visible count text.
- **Result rows:** one `Button` per `List` row (`PlayerSearchResultRows` for players). Songs show 40 pt decorative art (`ArtworkTile`, hidden from VoiceOver since #403), title and artist. A selected player's row opens Statistics.
